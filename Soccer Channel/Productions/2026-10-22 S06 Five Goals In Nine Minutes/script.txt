The player who scored five goals in nine minutes. Back in 2015, Bayern Munich were playing Wolfsburg, and Pep Guardiola left Robert Lewandowski on the bench. By half-time, Bayern were losing {1-0|one-nil} at home. So Guardiola sent Lewandowski on. Six minutes later, he slid in a goal from close range. 58 seconds after that, he scored again from outside the box. Two minutes later, he hit the post, the keeper saved the rebound, and Lewandowski smashed in a third. That was the fastest hat-trick in Bundesliga history, three minutes and 22 seconds. But he wasn't done. Two minutes later, he volleyed in a fourth. Then came a fifth, an acrobatic volley. Lewandowski said that after his fourth, he looked up at the big screen and just thought, oh. Five goals in eight minutes and 59 seconds. Guardiola said that neither as a player nor as a coach had he ever seen anything like it. And it almost got even crazier. Later in the game, Wolfsburg cleared one of his shots off the line. If it had gone in, he would have equalled the Bundesliga record of six goals in one game, set back in 1977. Bayern won {5-1|five-one}. But here's the wild part. A few weeks later, Guinness gave Lewandowski four world records for that one night: the fastest hat-trick, the fastest four goals, the fastest five goals, and the most goals ever by a substitute in the Bundesliga. All from a player who started the game on the bench.
