The team with worse odds than the Loch Ness monster. Back in 2015, Leicester City had almost been relegated from the Premier League. They were bottom at Christmas, and only survived by winning seven of their last nine games. So before the next season, bookmakers gave Leicester odds of {5,000-1|five thousand to one} to win the league. Bookmakers gave better odds on finding Elvis alive, and on finding the Loch Ness monster. And when one fan put a pound on Leicester in a betting shop, the manager wrote, pigs might fly, on his slip. Leicester hired Claudio Ranieri, a manager who had been sacked by Greece. But Leicester started winning, and they just didn't stop. They lost just three league games all season. Jamie Vardy scored in eleven Premier League games in a row, a new record. In February, they beat Manchester City {3-1|three-one} away. And the fans who had bet on them at five thousand to one started getting offers to cash out. One fan had put fifty pounds on Leicester, to win two hundred and fifty thousand. In March, he took seventy-two thousand instead. Then, on the 2nd of May 2016, Tottenham drew at Chelsea, and Leicester City were the champions of England. But here's the wild part. Ladbrokes had taken 47 bets at five thousand to one. Around half of those fans had already cashed out.
