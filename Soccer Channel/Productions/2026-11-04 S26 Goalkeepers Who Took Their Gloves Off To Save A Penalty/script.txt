Goalkeepers who took their gloves off to save a penalty. Back in {2004|two thousand and four}, Portugal played England in the quarter-final of the Euros, in Lisbon. It finished {2-2|two-all}, so it went to penalties. Beckham missed for England. Rui Costa missed for Portugal. It stayed tight, all the way to sudden death. Then, with the shootout in sudden death, Darius Vassell walked up to take England's kick. And Portugal's goalkeeper, Ricardo, did something strange. He tore off his gloves, and threw them to the side of the goal. Bare hands. Against a penalty. He jumped to his left, and saved it. Then Ricardo did something even stranger. He walked up to take the next penalty himself. He said he used to practise penalties, and it wasn't his first in a competitive match. He sized up David James, and hit it low, past him. {6-5|Six-five}. Portugal were through. Ricardo later said he saw Vassell coming, and decided on a little crazy act. Just mind games. He wanted extra motivation, and to confuse the taker. He was very happy it worked. Years later, they became team-mates at Leicester. And Vassell told him he was already nervous before the kick, and it got worse when the gloves came off.
