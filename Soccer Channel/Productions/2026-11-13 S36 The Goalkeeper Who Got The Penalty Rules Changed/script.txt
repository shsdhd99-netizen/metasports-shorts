The goalkeeper who got the penalty rules changed. And he says they changed them too late. Back in 2021, Emiliano Martínez faced Colombia in a Copa América shootout. Before Yerry Mina's kick, he got in his head. Mina missed. Argentina went through. A year later, the World Cup final against France went to penalties. Martínez saved one. Then, before Aurélien Tchouaméni's kick, he threw the ball away. Tchouaméni missed. Argentina were world champions. That March, football's lawmakers rewrote the penalty rule. From July 2023, a keeper can't unfairly distract the taker. Can't delay the kick. Can't even touch the posts, the crossbar or the net. Everyone called it the anti-Martínez rule. So what did he think? "They did it too late. We're already champions of everything."
