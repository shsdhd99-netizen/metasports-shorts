René {Higuita|Ee-ghee-tah} saved a shot at Wembley with his heels. Behind his head. In {1995|nineteen ninety-five}, England's Jamie Redknapp, on his debut, miscued a cross, and it floated straight at Colombia's keeper. Any keeper in the world just catches that. Higuita wasn't showing off. He'd been waiting for that ball for five years. He got the idea filming a soda advert, when a kid kicked a ball at him and he flicked it away with both heels. So he practised it. In training. In warm-ups. Never once in a match. Until that night. He let the ball drop over his head, dived forward, and kicked it clear, like a scorpion's tail. The commentator thought the whistle must have gone. England's coach, Bryan Robson, was laughing on the bench. The game finished nil-nil, so the save is all anyone remembers. But here's what nobody noticed. The linesman's flag was already up for offside. Play only carried on because the referee missed it. The save the whole world remembers never had to happen.
