Players who got sent off in their very last game. Back in 2006, Zinedine Zidane had already announced that the World Cup would be the last tournament of his career. And France made it all the way to the final against Italy, in Berlin. After seven minutes, Zidane stepped up to take a penalty against Gianluigi Buffon. But instead of smashing it, he chipped it down the middle. The ball hit the underside of the crossbar, bounced over the line, and France were winning. Twelve minutes later, Marco Materazzi headed in the equalizer. The game went to extra time, and Zidane almost won it with a header, but Buffon tipped it over the bar. Then, in the 110th minute, Zidane and Materazzi exchanged a few words. Zidane walked away. Then he turned around and headbutted Materazzi right in the chest. The referee hadn't even seen it. But the fourth official had, and Zidane was shown a red card. So one of the greatest players of all time walked off the pitch for the last time, right past the World Cup trophy. And without him, France lost the shootout {5-3|five-three}. Italy were world champions. But here's the wild part. The next morning, FIFA announced the best player of the whole tournament. Most of the journalists had voted before the final was over, before the headbutt. And the winner was Zinedine Zidane. The man who got sent off in the World Cup final was named the best player of the World Cup.
