Goalkeepers who hid a cheat sheet on their water bottle. Back in 2018, England played Colombia at the World Cup, and England had never won a World Cup penalty shootout. They lost in 1990, in 1998, and in 2006. Then, deep into stoppage time, Yerry Mina headed in an equalizer for Colombia. It went to penalties again. But England's goalkeeper, Jordan Pickford, had a secret. His water bottle had notes on it, showing where each Colombian player liked to shoot. And when he walked past Colombia's keeper, he covered it with a towel. England missed their third kick. But then Mateus Uribe smashed Colombia's next one onto the crossbar. And when Carlos Bacca stepped up, Pickford dived to his left, threw up his arm, and saved it. Eric Dier scored, and England had finally won a World Cup shootout. Afterwards, Pickford said only one Colombian, Radamel Falcao, had shot differently from what he expected. So Pickford kept the trick. In the Euro 2020 final against Italy, he had his bottle again, and he saved two penalties, but England still lost. But here's the wild part. This summer, England played Argentina in the World Cup semi-final, and Pickford had the bottle ready one more time. But Argentina scored in the 85th and the 92nd minute, so there was no shootout. And after the game, the Argentina players found his bottle on the pitch, and passed it to Messi. Next to one player's name, the note reportedly just said, feel on the day. And they couldn't stop laughing.
