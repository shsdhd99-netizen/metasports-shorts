The player who moved two Coca-Cola bottles at the Euros.

Back in {2021|twenty twenty-one}, Cristiano Ronaldo sat down for a press conference in Budapest, the day before Portugal's first game.

In front of him were two bottles of Coca-Cola.

And Coca-Cola was an official sponsor of the tournament.

Ronaldo looked at the bottles. He picked them up. And he moved them out of the camera's view.

Then he held up a bottle of water, and said one word: "Agua."

The clip went everywhere.

Headlines said Ronaldo had wiped billions off Coca-Cola.

But that story fell apart. Snopes found Coca-Cola's stock was already falling before he even sat down.

A tournament spokesperson simply said everyone is entitled to their drink preferences.

But the bottles kept moving.

At his own press conference, Paul Pogba saw a bottle of Heineken in front of him. He picked it up, and took it off the table.

Other players started joking about it, showing off Coke bottles of their own.

But here's the wild part.

Back in {2006|two thousand six}, Ronaldo appeared in a commercial for Coca-Cola.

The man who moved the bottles had once been on the cans.
