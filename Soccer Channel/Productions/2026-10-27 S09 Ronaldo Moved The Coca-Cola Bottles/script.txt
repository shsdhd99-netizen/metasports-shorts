Ronaldo moved two Coca-Cola bottles, and billions disappeared.

Back in June twenty twenty-one, Cristiano Ronaldo walked into a press conference in Budapest.

It was the day before Portugal's first game at Euro twenty twenty.

On the table in front of him were two bottles of Coca-Cola.

Coca-Cola was an official sponsor of the tournament.

Ronaldo looked at the bottles. He picked them up. And he moved them out of the camera's view.

Then he held up a bottle of water, and said one word: "Agua."

But the clip went everywhere.

Headlines said Ronaldo had wiped four billion dollars off Coca-Cola.

The numbers looked real. Coca-Cola's market value did go from about two hundred forty-two billion to two hundred thirty-eight billion.

But here is the part nobody mentions.

Snopes found the stock was already falling before he sat down, with no sign he caused any of it.

Coca-Cola said everyone is entitled to their drink preferences. The tournament said players are offered water, Coke, or Coke Zero when they arrive.

And the next night, Ronaldo played Hungary.

Portugal won three-nil. Ronaldo scored twice, in the last minutes, and became the Euros' all-time top scorer.

One man moved two bottles, and the internet gave him the credit for billions.

And nobody has proved he cost Coca-Cola a cent.
