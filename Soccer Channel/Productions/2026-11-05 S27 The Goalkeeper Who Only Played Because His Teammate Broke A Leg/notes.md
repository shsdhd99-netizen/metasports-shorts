# S27 The goalkeeper who only played because his teammate broke a leg (story, 54.1 s)

- **Lane:** goalkeeper-in-a-shootout series. Odd device = he was never meant to play (a teammate's broken leg). Idea gauge GO (72%).
- **Concept (8 words):** Understudy keeper replaces injured Pumpido, saves penalties.
- **Added value:** the ending flips the opening device: the man who only played by accident saved four to reach the final and lost it to one penalty. No money line, one timeline, no promo.
- **Copy-check:** goalkeeper-shootout lane, not a copy of any Ahmed story.
- **Numbers in the voice:** eleven (minutes), four (saves), one-nil. Year 1990.
