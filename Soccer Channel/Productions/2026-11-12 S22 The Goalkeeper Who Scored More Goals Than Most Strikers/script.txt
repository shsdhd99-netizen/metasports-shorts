The goalkeeper who scored more goals than most strikers. And he was the first keeper ever to reach a hundred. Rogério Ceni played in goal for São Paulo for twenty-two years. In 1997 he started taking their free kicks. Then their penalties. By 2006 he was one goal from the world record for a goalkeeper. Against Cruzeiro, he saved a penalty. A few minutes later, he scored a free kick to break the record. Then he scored a penalty in the same game. In 2011, he curled a free kick into the top corner against Corinthians. Goal number one hundred. He retired in 2015 with a hundred and thirty-one goals. Sixty-nine penalties, sixty-one free kicks, and one in open play. But the best part is 2005. That year São Paulo won the Copa Libertadores and the Club World Cup, and their top scorer wasn't a striker. It was the goalkeeper. Twenty-one goals.
