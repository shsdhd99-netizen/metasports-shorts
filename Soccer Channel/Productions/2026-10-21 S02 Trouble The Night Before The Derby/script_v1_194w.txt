Players who got in trouble the day before a derby. Back in October 2011, Manchester City were about to play Manchester United at Old Trafford, and up front for City was Mario Balotelli. But the day before the derby, just before 1 a.m., two fire crews were called to his house. A firework had gone off in his bathroom, and the room was on fire. Within hours, it was all over the news. But Roberto Mancini still put him in the team. And after 22 minutes, Balotelli scored. Then he lifted his shirt over his head to show a message underneath. Why always me? The referee booked him for it. But it got even crazier. In the second half, Balotelli scored again, and City just kept going. Three, four, five, six. Manchester City won {6-1|six-one}, United's worst home defeat since 1955. But here's the wild part. That shirt had nothing to do with the fire. Balotelli asked the kit man to print it on the Monday before the derby, days before any fire. And two days after the derby, the man whose bathroom had caught fire was telling Manchester to follow the firework code.
