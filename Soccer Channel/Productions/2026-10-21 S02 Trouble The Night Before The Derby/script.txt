Players who got in trouble the day before a derby. Back in October 2011, Manchester City were about to play Manchester United at Old Trafford, and up front for City was Mario Balotelli. But just before 1 a.m. on the day before the derby, two fire crews were called to his house. Balotelli had four friends over, and they were letting off fireworks out of the bathroom window. The towels caught fire, and the bathroom went up in flames. Firefighters had to go in with breathing apparatus. And reports say Balotelli even ran back inside to grab a suitcase and some cash, so the police had to make sure he stayed out. By the morning, it was all over the news. But Balotelli checked into a hotel, and at 10 a.m. he turned up for training like nothing had happened. And Roberto Mancini still put him in the team. After 22 minutes, Balotelli scored. Then he lifted his shirt over his head to show a message underneath. Why always me? The referee booked him for it. But it got even crazier. In the second half, Balotelli scored again, and City just kept going. Three, four, five, six. Manchester City won {6-1|six-one}, United's worst home defeat since 1955, and City went five points clear at the top. But here's the wild part. That shirt had nothing to do with the fire. Balotelli asked the kit man to print it on the Monday before the derby, days before any fire. And the day after the derby, the man whose bathroom had caught fire was telling Manchester to follow the firework code.
