The Ballon d'Or winner who missed a penalty days later. Last month, Harry Kane won the Ballon d'Or, beating Kylian {Mbappe|Em-bap-ay} by just twenty-eight points. Five days later, Bayern Munich showed his trophy to their fans, right before Der Klassiker: Bayern against Dortmund. Kane even walked out on his own, separate from the other players. Dortmund thought they'd scored first, but VAR ruled Jobe Bellingham offside. Then Bayern took the lead. Then, early in the second half, Bayern got a penalty. Kane, who almost never misses, stepped up, and hit the crossbar. Three minutes later, he lost the ball in the build-up, and Dortmund equalised. Kane tried to fix it. He set up Dayot {Upamecano|Oo-pah-meh-kah-no}, and Bayern led again. Then, in the last minute, a Dortmund substitute, Fabio Silva, scored. With the last kick of the game, Bayern's Lennart Karl hit the crossbar too. Draw. Bayern missed the chance to pull clear of Dortmund at the top. But here's the wild part. Kane had missed only two of his twenty-seven Bundesliga penalties. One of them came on the night he showed the fans his Ballon d'Or.
