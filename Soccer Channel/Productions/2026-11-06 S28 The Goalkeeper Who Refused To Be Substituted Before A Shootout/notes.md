# S28 The goalkeeper who refused to be substituted before a shootout (story, 51.9 s)

- **Lane:** goalkeeper-in-a-shootout series. Odd device = he waved away the substitution meant for a shootout specialist. Idea gauge GO (76%).
- **Concept (8 words):** Kepa refused to leave before the shootout, lost anyway.
- **Added value:** the ending flips the opening device: he would not make way for the specialist who won this same trophy on penalties for City, saved one, and lost to that man's old club. One timeline, no money line, no promo.
