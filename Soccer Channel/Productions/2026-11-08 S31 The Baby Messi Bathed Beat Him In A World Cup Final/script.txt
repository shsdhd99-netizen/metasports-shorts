The baby Messi bathed beat him in a World Cup final. Back in {2007|two thousand seven}, a Barcelona newspaper made a charity calendar. Local families entered a raffle to be in it, next to a Barcelona player. One family from {Mataro|Mah-tah-roh} won, and on the day, they were paired with Messi by chance. Their son was a few months old. So a smiling Messi gave the baby a bath, in a little plastic tub. Years later, the baby's dad posted the photos, with a caption: the beginning of two legends. The baby was Lamine Yamal. He grew up to wear Messi's old number ten at Barcelona. But the two of them had never played against each other. Until this summer, in the World Cup final. Argentina against Spain. Messi became the oldest outfield player ever to play in one. It went to extra time, and Spain won it. At the final whistle, Yamal went to a tearful Messi, and they hugged. Yamal says Messi told him to keep going his own way, and that the future belongs to his generation. But here's the wild part. Argentina came into that final as world champions. And the baby Messi once held in a plastic tub is the one who took the title off him.
