The manager Barcelona soaked with sprinklers just got his revenge. Back in {2010|twenty ten}, José Mourinho's Inter knocked Barcelona out of the Champions League, at Camp Nou. Inter lost the game {1-0|one-nil}, but went through on aggregate. Mourinho sprinted onto the pitch with his finger in the air. So Barcelona's ground staff turned the sprinklers on. He didn't care. He kept celebrating in the spray. Weeks later, he won the final and became Real Madrid's manager. He left in {2013|twenty thirteen}. But this summer, he came back. In September, before his first Champions League game back, an Italian journalist handed him a gift. A garden sprinkler, with a bow on it. Then came Sunday. His first Clásico back, at Camp Nou. Barcelona had won all nine league games. One more win would break a record set in {1968|nineteen sixty-eight}, by Real Madrid. Madrid's goalkeeper, Thibaut Courtois, had the flu. Vinícius was suspended. Mbappé scored. Raphinha equalised. Then, with time running out, Mbappé scored again. His hundredth goal for Real Madrid. And Mourinho dropped to his knees on the touchline, just like that night in {2010|twenty ten}. But here's the wild part. The record Barcelona were chasing belonged to Real Madrid. And the team that stopped them was Real Madrid.
