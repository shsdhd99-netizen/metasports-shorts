## 1. The England player who kept Maradona's Hand of God shirt
- **Title (line 1):** "The England player who kept Maradona's Hand of God shirt." Title card: `The England player who kept\NMaradona's HAND OF GOD shirt`.
- **Premise (beats):**
  1. Back in 1986, Maradona faced England in the World Cup quarter-final.
  2. Turn: an England midfielder, Steve Hodge, tried a back-pass, and Maradona punched it in.
  3. Escalation: four minutes later, Maradona scored the Goal of the Century.
  4. Twist: in the tunnel, Hodge asked Maradona to swap shirts.
  5. He lent it to the National Football Museum, where it was on display from 2002.
  6. Payoff: in 2022 it sold for £7,142,500, a world record for sports memorabilia at the time.
- **Key facts:**
  1. Match date, and the back-pass that set up the goal. FIFA (inside.fifa.com): https://inside.fifa.com/news/hodge-maradona-and-the-hand-of-god-shirt-2801683. Quote: "Unaware of Maradona's run, Hodge attempted a back-pass to goalkeeper Peter Shilton." Also: "Sunday, 22 June 1986".
  2. The second goal came four minutes later. Wikipedia, "The hand of God": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_hand_of_God. Quote: "Four minutes after the goal gave Argentina a 1–0 lead".
  3. The shirt swap, in Hodge's own words (FIFA, as in fact 1). Quote: "tugged on my shirt as if to say 'any chance of swapping?'"
  4. Twenty years in a museum (FIFA, as in fact 1). Quote: "on a long-term loan to England's National Football Museum, on display since 2002".
  5. The sale. LBC, May 2022: https://lbc.co.uk/news/diego-maradona-hand-of-god-shirt-sells-at-auction. Quote: "£7,142,500" ("Sotheby's arranged the auction"). Wikipedia (as in fact 2): "a world record for a piece of sports memorabilia".
- **Note:** say "a world record **at the time**". A Babe Ruth jersey went for more in 2024; check the figure before using it.
- **Optional extra beat:** the match ball, owned by the referee Ali Bin Nasser, later sold for £2m. Gulf Today, Nov 16 2022: https://gulftoday.ae/sport/2022/11/16/maradona-hand-of-god-ball-fetches-2-million-at-auction. Quote: "sold at an auction for £2 million ($2.4 million)".
- **Ahmed's Goal title grep:** no title mentions Maradona, Hand of God, Hodge or a shirt auction. "BIGGEST ROBBERIES IN FOOTBALL HISTORY" (2M) probably shows the handball, but not the shirt.
- **Footage lead:** the 1986 quarter-final broadcast (check archive.org); news photos of the shirt at Sotheby's (2022); Hodge with the shirt.

## 2. Players who got revenge on the team that got them sent off (Beckham, 1998 -> 2002)
