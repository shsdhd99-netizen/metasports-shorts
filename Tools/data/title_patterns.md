# Title & Thumbnail Patterns (data-backed)

*Updated 2026-09-26.*

**Sources:**
- all 432 CoachClank Shorts and your 80 MetaSports Shorts (public view counts)
- the breakout videos of Pauly Walnuts and 6 peer commentary channels (Round 2 research)

Numbers are medians, so one freak viral hit can't skew them.

## MetaSports — what 432 CoachClank titles say

| Title feature | Median views | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **6–7 words** (140 Shorts) | **694K** | best common length |
| 4–5 words (200) | 423K | fine |
| 8–9 words (49) | 239K | worst — too long |
| **Has a question mark** (50) | **847K** vs 480K without | biggest single lift |
| Starts with *What* / *How* (12 / 5) | 2.05M / 2.6M | small sample, huge upside |
| Starts with *Why* (41) | 528K | solid |
| Starts with *This…* (22) | 174K | avoid |
| 1 emoji (85) | 553K vs 456K without | small lift, optional |
| An ALL-CAPS word (22) | 367K vs 523K | avoid |
| A number (48) | 425K vs 518K | numbers alone don't sell |
| Hashtags in the title | **0 of 432** | CoachClank never does it |

**Your 80 titles:**
- 78 have hashtags stuck in the title (`#NBA #Shorts`…).
- They average 6.3 words.
- Your views barely change with any title feature; everything sits near 30K.

So the topic and the first 2 seconds are what cap you, not the title. Still, drop the hashtags. It costs nothing, and it's what CoachClank does (median 510K views per Short).

### The 5 title rules
1. **4–7 words.** Never 8+.
2. **No hashtags and no description.** Clean title only.
3. **Ask it.** "What / How / Why ___?" beats a statement.
4. **One reaction emoji at the end is optional** (😳 🤯 😭 👀 🤔). It's the viewer's face, not decoration.
5. **Don't start with "This…"** and don't use ALL-CAPS words.

### Fill-in-the-blank templates
- What Happened To ___?
- How Did ___ Get Away With It?
- Why ___ Never ___ Again?
- Why Nobody Guards ___?
- ___ Broke The NBA's ___ Rule?
- Nobody Expected ___ To ___
- The ___ Nobody Talks About

## Commentary — what broke small channels out (Round 2)

| Pattern | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Why I ___** (a confession), then a **Part 2** when it hits | real stakes; Part 2s ride the same audience |
| **___ Are a Humiliation Ritual** | names a shared frustration bluntly |
| **Life as a ___ after ___** (a life stage) | the viewer sees himself in the title |
| **The Truth About ___ Nowadays** | promises honesty |
| **Why Modern Life Feels So ___** | puts words to a vague feeling |
| **They Lied to Us About ___** | us-vs-them; keep the video hopeful |
| **I Realized Too Late ___** | regret plus a lesson |
| A callout of a trend or creator (rarely) | pulls attention, but stick to facts |

**Rules:**
- About 5 words.
- It should read like a text to a friend; lowercase-casual is fine.
- **Thumbnail text ≠ title.** The 2–3 thumbnail words must add something the title doesn't say.

## Thumbnails

- **Commentary:**
  - The layout: a gameplay background, one character cutout with a white sticker outline, and 2–3 huge words.
  - It must be readable on a phone at arm's length.
  - Make 3 versions with `/thumbnail`, which uses `thumb.py --variants 3`. Upload all 3 in YouTube Studio → **Test & Compare**.
- **Cutouts for free:** open the picture in **Windows Paint → Remove background**, then save it as PNG.
- **MetaSports:**
  - In the Shorts feed, the first frame is what sells. Put the moment on screen from second 1.
  - A 9:16 cover (`--shape short`) only helps on your channel page and in search. Use it if your YouTube app offers a custom Shorts thumbnail.
