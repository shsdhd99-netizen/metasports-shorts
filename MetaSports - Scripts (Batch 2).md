### 2 · Thu Oct 1 · Is the Free Throw Line Lying? 😳

> Every NBA free throw is shot from 15 feet… except it isn't. You see, the rulebook measures that line from the backboard, not from the rim. The rim sits 6 inches off the glass, and it's 18 inches wide, so the middle of the hoop is 15 inches in front of the backboard. Which means every free throw is really 13 feet, 9 inches. But here's the weird part. The three-point line IS measured from the middle of the basket. So the two most important lines in basketball are measured from two different places. And some of the biggest guys in the league still can't make it from 13 feet 9.

### 6 · Mon Oct 5 · Why Is the Corner Three Shorter? 🧠

> The corner three is the biggest cheat code in basketball, and it's all because of math. At the top of the key, the three-point line is 23 feet, 9 inches from the basket. But in the corner, it's only 22 feet. You see, the court is 50 feet wide, so the hoop is 25 feet from each sideline. If the arc kept going all the way around, there'd barely be a foot of room to stand. So the NBA chops it off with straight lines, 3 feet from each sideline. Same three points, almost two feet closer. That's why every smart team hunts the corner, and why the best shooters live there.
