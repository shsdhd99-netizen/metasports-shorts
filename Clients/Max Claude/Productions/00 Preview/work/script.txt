Here's something you do a hundred times a day without noticing. Every time you touch something, you leave your signature behind. Your fingerprints formed before you were born, and they will never change. Not even identical twins share them. Each one is a pattern of ridges that split and stop, and those tiny details are what make it yours. Touch a glass, and the moisture and oil leave a copy. An investigator brushes on a fine powder, and the invisible print appears. A computer searches a database of stored prints and suggests possible matches. For crime scene prints, a trained examiner usually makes the final call. So the next time you open a door, remember: you just signed your name.
