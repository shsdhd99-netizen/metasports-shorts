Here's something you do a hundred times a day without noticing. Every time you touch something, you leave your signature behind. Your fingerprints formed before you were born, and they will never change. Not even identical twins share them. Each one is a pattern of ridges that loop, split and stop, and those tiny details are what make it yours. Touch a glass, and the oils on your skin leave a perfect copy. An investigator brushes on a fine powder, and the invisible print appears. A computer can compare it against millions of prints in seconds. But the computer only suggests a match. A trained examiner makes the final call. So the next time you open a door, remember: you just signed your name.
