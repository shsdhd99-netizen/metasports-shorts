Your one-time recording

Ten minutes of prep for studio quality.

Voice

  • A quiet room with soft things around (a closet with clothes works)
  • A USB or lapel mic, a hand's width from your mouth
  • Read the way you teach; vary your energy
  • 30 minutes minimum; 1–2 hours sounds best

Face

  • Camera at eye level, 4K if you can, steady
  • Light in front of you, plain background
  • Talk to the lens for 2–5 minutes
  • Close your lips between sentences

Not sure what to say? Read this aloud.

"Hi, I'm Max. Today I want to explain something most people get wrong. It sounds simple, but the details matter. First, let's look at where it starts. Then I'll show you the part that surprised me most. By the end, you'll see it everywhere."

Read it in a few moods, then any book or article for the rest. You'll also record a short consent clip; the voice and avatar services require it.
