How a computer made a video of you.

No camera crew and no editing software on your side. Three separate jobs were done by three separate tools, and we put them together and checked the result.

1 · THE VOICE

It learned your voice

You sent a voice memo of about a minute. A voice program listened and learned how you sound, a bit like a very good impressionist. Then it read our script out loud in that voice.

2 · THE FACE

It animated your photo

You sent one photo. A second program moves the face in that photo (lips, cheeks, a little head motion) so the mouth matches the words. A third step fine-tunes the lips so they line up with each sound.

3 · THE WRITING

We wrote and checked it

We wrote the script, checked every fact against reliable sources, picked the pictures, added captions and music, and made sure the length was right. You did none of that.

Is it really "you"? It is your photo, a computer copy of your voice, and words you can approve. You never recorded those lines. That is why every video needs your approval before it goes anywhere, and why the small "AI" label in the corner stays on. We think being open about it is the right way to do this.

Questions people ask

The honest answers.

Is this the final quality?

No. It is a first draft made for free, so you can judge the idea. The table above shows what gets better with a budget: a cleaner voice copy, a face built from a short video of you, a sharper picture and custom visuals.

What will it cost?

We will tell you the price in writing before you pay anything. Nothing on this page charges you.

Where does this video get posted?

Nowhere unless you say so. This page is private, and students, parents and anyone else only see what you approve.

Will it really look and sound like me?

The cloned voice is usually very close to the real thing. The face depends on what we start from: a quick preview made from one photo is good, and the full version, made from a 5-minute video of you, is much more natural. You see it before you pay for anything beyond setup.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. You record once (we send you simple step-by-step instructions), then you just send topics. Everything technical is our job.

Is my face and voice safe?

They're only used to make videos you've approved. The voice and face models are kept in accounts in your name, so you own them. If you ever stop, we delete our copies.

Who owns the videos?

You do. You get the files and can use them in class, online, or anywhere else.

What if I don't like a video?

Tell us what to change. Nothing is final until you say it is.

Should I tell my students it's AI?

We recommend it, and it can be a lesson in itself. Each video can carry a small "made with AI, approved by Mr. Claude" note if you like.

How long does a video take?

Usually 3–4 days from your topic to the finished video.
