## 3. Diagnosis

### 3.1 The backlog drove the Oct 17-20 dip [V]
- **Scripted before RULE 1 (Oct 19):** #46, #45 and #10, all scripted Oct 15-16.
- **Scripted in lanes RULE 1 then retired:** #49 and #53.
- **Meanwhile, the format posts waited:** #48, #51 and #52 sat on footage and approval while the owner drained this queue.
- **So the format itself is untested** (2 posts, no stayed data yet). It can't be blamed yet.

### 3.3 The format clones drift from #42 [V text / H effect]
**#48 Harden:**
- **The clash goes missing:**
  - LeBron, the all-time scorer, owning the most misses fights his image.
  - Harden is known for taking huge numbers of threes, so owning the most missed threes confirms his [H].
- **The paradox line is weaker:**
  - #42: "all-time leading scorer" (#1 against #1).
  - #48: "more threes than anyone except Steph Curry" (a qualified #2). Curry is also #2 in misses, so two names crowd both lists.
- **The human beat is gone:** "the same night he first played with his son" became another stat ("1,028 threes" in a season).
- **The flip is qualified:** "anyone not named Curry: 9,323" against #42's "more than anyone, ever: 31,502".
- **Early pace:** 1,600 at about 1 day, against #42's about 3,100 at the same age [V counts; H meaning].

**#64 Wemby:**
- **Frame 1 is a face:** #64 v2 opens on a close-up of Wemby's face (QA swap, Oct 21, UPLOAD.md). The spec says frame 1 shows the bad thing, the way #42 opened on the airball [V].
- **The measure drifts:** shooting (1-for-13), then the DPOY award (defense), then rebounds and blocks. #42 stays on shooting throughout [V].
- **Too many numbers:** 9 spoken numbers in 72 words, against #42's 6 in 75 [V]. The last number-heavy script, #29 (8 numbers), got 1.9K and 59% stayed [S].

## 4. Rules for future NBA scripts
1. **The accusation clashes with the star's reputation on the same stat, ideally #1 vs #1.** The test: would a casual fan say "no way"?
   - Evidence: #42 (scorer/misser, 84.3%), #22, #03.
   - #48's confirming accusation trails #42's day-1 pace [S/H].
2. **One measure from line 1 to the end.** The flip is one unqualified superlative number on that measure: never "except" or "not named".
   - Worked: #42, #22, #19, #58.
   - Didn't: #64 (3 measures), #29 (5 measures, 59%) [S].
3. **Present-tense facts only.** No "can / could / will he", no "did the NBA plan".
   - Evidence: #46 49.8%, #21 63.7%, #10 4.6K [S].
4. **No rule, policy or bookkeeping premises, even behind an "only" or "nobody" opener.**
   - Evidence: lane median 2.1K. #53, #45, #14, #20 and #11 sit at 45-3.5K; #40 needed a duplicate upload to reach 6.7K [V].
5. **Space the stars.** No more than 1 LeBron or Bronny post in any 4, and never the same star twice in 48 h.
   - Evidence: 5 of 9 posts Oct 16-20; the LeBron posts after #42 got 3.2-6.8K [S].
   - Exception: fresh news with a number gap (#58).
6. **Frame 1 is the surprising thing happening, not a face.**
   - Worked: #42's airball (84.3%), #22's jersey, #19's height shown with scale.
   - Didn't: #46, LeBron's face for 57% of the runtime (49.8%) [S].
   - Revert #64's face opener in future builds.
7. **At most 6 spoken numbers, one per sentence, and keep the human beat.**
   - Evidence: #42 used 6 numbers; #29 used 8 (59%); #64 uses 9.
   - Human and legacy endings earned the most subs (#03 16, #23 13) [S].
   - Trim #60, which has 13 numbers, before posting.
8. **News only if it's under 36 h old *and* carries a number gap about a famous name.**
   - Worked: #58 17K, #23 10K.
   - Didn't: #45 (a June story) 3.5K, #49 (2024) 5.3K, #10 (a preview) 4.6K [S].

**First:** export Studio → Content → Shorts. The stayed % for #48 and #64 decides the format test (2 of 3 at 74%+), and nothing posted since #46 has been measured.
