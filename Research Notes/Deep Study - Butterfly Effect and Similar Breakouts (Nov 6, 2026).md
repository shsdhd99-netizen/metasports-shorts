## 3. Devices to test (one variable each; log in `Test Log.csv`)
1. **Cause and consequence in line 1:** "If it weren't for [tiny thing], [famous outcome] would never have happened." Our tension-first hook in its strongest form.
2. **The loop ending:** the last line is unfinished and leads into line 1 (theirs: "So tell me..." into "Did you know..."). It conflicts with our "never end on a question" rule (Oct 13 data). So test it **only** as a loop line, not as a question to the viewer, 2 Shorts vs 2.
3. **A year tag plus a club-logo transition** at each link of the chain. Cheap, and it keeps a multi-step story easy to follow.
4. **Colour-highlighted words in captions** (already our caption A/B: `--color-rule word`).
