## D. Pre-publication check

`qa_short.py` stays the technical gate. It is not this check. Before a file is called postable, look at the export:

1. Pause at 0.2s. The unusual fact is visible without the caption. A name, a team, or "the NBA" as the only information on screen fails.
2. The number the sentence depends on is one caption card, on the matching person. "NINE" fails when the fact is 209.
3. The picture does not contradict the line. A packed arena under "only nine" fails.
4. The twist is not saved for the last sentence after a history list.
5. The story is not a salary, a third visit to a posted family, or a star's schedule. Duren, the pay cut, the helicopter, and the two Bronny posts already ran those.
6. No top headline. This is the house rule. Flagg shows it is not, by itself, the reason a video lives or dies.
7. No effect in the first second. An effect only where the frame makes that sound. Zero effects is allowed.
8. Play the last two seconds. The last line is still true on the looped picture.
9. `qa_short.py` prints PASS. Then say, in one line, which posted video this one is actually like, using the three numbers above. "It passed QA" is not that line.

Verdicts: PASS only when those nine are true on the export. NEEDS REVISION when the premise is worth it and a named frame fails. REJECT when the premise is a salary, a repeat family, or a fact stack whose twist is the last line.
