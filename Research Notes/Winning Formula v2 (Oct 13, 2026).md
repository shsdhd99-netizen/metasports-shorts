## (c) The formula
1. **Story type: one visible object, one paradox.** A non-fan can see the thing (a jersey number, a height, a shooting form, a vote) and it "can't be right"; resolve it in 20-24 s. This covers all 3 new-era breakouts and 6 of 9 old ones (free throw, underhand, dunk without jumping, "200 IQ", "cheat code", stopping Curry). [V count, I rule]
2. **Curiosity:** "how is that possible?", not "what's next?" (news) or "was it worth it?" (contracts, picks). [I]
3. **Second 1:** the object fills the frame and the first sentence states the paradox in plain words. No insider opener ("Knicks center... playoff free throws", "Ninety-two point nine... Bad Boy Pistons", "A guaranteed deal..."). [I]
4. **New fact every 2-3 s, all on the same measure** (heights, owners of the number, voters). Don't hop between stats. [I]
5. **Replayable ending:** the last line answers the first, ideally in the subject's own voice or as one number, over the opening image. All 3 Shorts above 19K had 100%+ avg viewed, but #14 (116%) and #01 (103%) stayed under 4K, so retention is needed, not enough. [V]
6. **Support:**
   - Red circle or arrow on the detail in frame 1. Old era: 7 of 27 such Shorts reached 40K+, vs 2 of 44 without (Fisher p≈0.02; within Jan-Feb, 6/23 vs 0/19). [V, coded by me alone] None of our 16 new first frames has one. [V]
   - Premise pill on screen (Flagg, n=1). [H]
   - No dark card in the first 3 s. [I]
   - Caption style is not decisive. [I]

**Anti-patterns:**
- Contract or pick premises: #12 2.9K, #13 1.9K, #01 3.9K, #24 5.7K. [V]
- Using a niche player as the way into a universal question (#29). [I]
- Debate-question or call-to-action endings (#12, #18, both under 3K). [V]
- Hedged endings: #25 "A little, maybe", 68.4% viewed. [V]
- Re-posts: #25 got 177 views. [V]
- Using the critic's ranking to decide posting order (see g).

## (e) Falsifiers
- The next 6 Shorts that meet ingredients 1-6 have a median under 5K at 48 h.
- Contract or role-player Shorts pass 18K while formula Shorts don't.
- Opening with a pointer gains 2 points or less in "stayed to watch" across 3 pairs.
- Every type of Short recovers at once. That would mean the drop came from distribution, not the premise.

## (f) Experiments (decide in advance, change one thing, post in the same slot)
1. **Pointer + pill:** 3 pairs of paradox Shorts; in each pair one opens with a red circle and a 1-line pill, the other plain.
   - Metrics: stayed-to-watch at 24 h, views at 48 h.
   - Success: the pointer version wins by 5+ points in 2 of 3 pairs, with median views at least 2x.
   - Failure: it wins by less than 2 points, or loses, in 2 of 3 pairs.
2. **Universal vs insider premise on the same star:** classify each Short before posting by asking a non-fan to state the question after 1 s.
   - Success: the universal version gets 2x the views at 48 h in 3 of 3 pairs.
   - Failure: it wins in 1 pair or fewer.
3. **Rating test:** for the next 10 posts, record three predictions before posting: the critic's craft score, a new 3-point score (items 3, 4 and 6 above) and the owner's gut ranking.
   - Success: the new score's ρ with 48 h views is 0.5 or more while craft's is 0.2 or less. Then drop craft from posting-order decisions.
   - Failure: the new score's ρ is below 0.3.
