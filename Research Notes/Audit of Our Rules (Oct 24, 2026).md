# Audit of our rules (Oct 24, 2026)

**What I did:** read every rulebook (`cloud-short`, `nba-short`, `story-short` + FORMAT, `titles`, `daily-run`, `Library/THE 42 FORMAT.md`,
the NBA Diagnosis, Lessons Learned) and tested every rule I could against our own numbers: all four Studio exports merged
(117 NBA uploads; the 38 posted Oct 3-21 since the channel restart have "stayed to watch") and the 3 soccer Shorts.
Outside research (niche studies, platform mechanics and policy) is in the other Oct 24 notes; this file is about our own evidence.

**Tiers:** [V] measured in our data · [S] seen a few times · [H] a guess · n = number of Shorts.

## 2. Keep: the evidence holds
1. **"Stayed to watch" is the number to optimise** [V]. Every craft rule should serve the first 1-2 seconds.
2. **Household-name star + one-line paradox** [V n=6/6 of the 20K+]. THE #42 FORMAT is one way to deliver it, not the only one (#22 "Six", the Wemby vote and Flagg's height were not #42 scripts).
3. **News only while fresh** [S].
4. **No question or CTA ending; end on the payoff** [S, Oct 13 data; Ahmed 19/19].
5. **Every fact sourced, exact words in notes.md**: protects us under YouTube's inauthentic-content and misinformation rules, and the owner's trust.
6. **QA gates**: frame 1 lit, sound dies away, captions match, loudness. Cheap, and each one exists because a real Short failed.

## 3. Wrong or outdated: fix now
1. **`cloud-short`: "[PROVEN] Lane: rule/ban/'caught'... 2.5x the channel median (n=26)".** That came from the old era and
   other channels. In our restart era, rule/policy Shorts are our weakest lane (63.7%, 2.0K, n=6). The NBA Diagnosis already
   retired the lane. **Relabel as outdated; demote.**
2. **`nba-short`: "argument-starting ending" (in the skill's description)** contradicts its own body ("No question ending") and the
   Oct 13 evidence. **Fix the description.**
3. **Numbers that disagree between files**: speech pace (240-290 vs 230-270 wpm; the house voice reads about 225), length
   (21+ / 22-30 / 23-40 / 20-30 s), "3+ cuts in the first 3 s" (NBA) vs "hold the first picture 2.5 s" (story), "last shot reuses
   the first clip (loop)" vs "end on the payoff picture". Our data says length doesn't matter in 20-33 s, and no loop or cut-rate
   rule has ever been tested here. **Make one table with one number each and its tier.**
4. **Rules adopted from one Short.** The "singular title" rule came from S01 alone and Ahmed's 1,355 Shorts contradicted it.
   **New bar: a rule needs 3+ consistent Shorts of ours or a strong outside source; until then it is a test.**
5. **`titles`: scored on CoachClank's 432 Shorts from another era.** Its "+1 for no hashtags" clashes with our posted titles
   (which carry #nba). Our own question-title lean is small. **Keep as a brainstorm aid, not a gate.**

## 5. Missing: things we don't do that the evidence points to
1. **One growing dataset.** Results sit in five places (Studio zips, trackers, Hub cards, Diagnosis tables). Decisions should come
   from one table: every Short's features (lane, star, fresh/evergreen, length, frame 1, music, caption style, title shape) next
   to its results (stayed %, avg % viewed, views at 7 days, subs). **Build it; the daily runs append to it.**
2. **Cross-posting.** Ahmed posts the same file to TikTok and Instagram (all his videos are over 1 minute because TikTok pays from
   1 minute). We post YouTube only. **An owner decision; noted, not done.**
3. **Re-test winners.** A Short that held 80%+ but stalled (Jokic pass) earned 4x more views on a second upload. Identical
   re-uploads risk "reused content", and the #25 re-upload got 197 views, so the safer test is a **new edit of the same story**
   (new frame 1, new title), not the same file.
4. **Series.** Ahmed reuses a title that works for new stories ("Episode 2...12", 141 Goal titles). Our "Who has missed the most..."
   pair (#42, #48) is a series by accident. **Name it and keep it going with superstar records.**
