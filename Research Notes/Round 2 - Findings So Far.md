Your Shorts retold stories Coach Clank had already told. **The gap is execution and being first/fresh, not topic choice.**

**Coach Clank's script DNA:**
1. **Opening:** a scene, a rule or a strong opinion ("They were making fun of this player…", "In the NBA there's an unwritten rule…", "James Harden has to be the funniest player of all time").
2. **The turn:** "But / However / You see…"
3. **The ending:** a consequence or punchline that sparks an argument. Viewers argued in the replies; the top comment had 48K likes.

**AI voice is not the problem.** 0 of 180 top comments mentioned the voice.

**Corrections to Round 1:**
- "Go 35–55s" was only half right: 21–45s is the sweet spot here.
- "Slow the voice to ~170 words/min" was wrong: this niche runs ~250+.
- "Descriptions matter" was wrong: the top channel uses none.
- "Subscriber conversion is the main problem" was wrong: this niche converts low. **Reach per Short is the problem.**

**Creator "advice" videos checked:** mostly course and tool sellers. One honest point worth keeping: you can't build a living on other people's footage and music alone.
