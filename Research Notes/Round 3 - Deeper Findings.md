# Round 3: Deeper Findings (Sep 27, 2026)

*This covers 431 CoachClank Shorts with exact upload times, from May 31, 2025 to Aug 27, 2026. All numbers are medians, so one freak hit can't skew them. Times are US Eastern.*

## 1. Length: 30–45 seconds wins, in both years

| Length | 2025 (n) | 2026 (n) |
|---|---|---|
| 20s or less | 738K (115) | 301K (123) |
| 21–30s | 279K (46) | 666K (74) |
| **31–45s** | **1.43M (27)** | **999K (17)** |
| 46–60s | 1.62M (17) | 49K (3) |
| over 60s | 151K (9) | — |

- **Caveat:** his longer Shorts carry his bigger stories, so part of this is the topic and not the length.
- **The pattern:** 30–45s is his best bucket in both years, and his clips of 20s or less were his weakest format in 2026.
- **New rule:**
  - Stories run 22–40s (about 90–170 words). Section 8 shows why: his biggest 2026 Shorts ran 22–34s.
  - Quick moments stay at 15–25s.
  - Never go over 60s: YouTube blocks Content ID-claimed Shorts over a minute, and his own 60s+ Shorts flopped.
- **Your own 3 Shorts over 30s** all flopped (~400 views). That's only 3 videos, and one was a fight clip, so it isn't proof. `/channel-check` will settle it within 2 weeks.

## 3. The day of the week doesn't matter

His best days flipped between years: Sun/Mon in 2025, Thu/Sat in 2026. Post every day.

## 5. Consistency beats everything, and bad months are normal

- **He never stopped.** He posted 25–34 Shorts every month from June 2025 to July 2026 without a break.
- **Even he swings hard month to month.** His monthly median went from 87K (Feb 2026) to 1.05M (Jun 2026). Judge yourself by 4-week medians, never by one week.
- **His best months weren't all in-season.** Jul and Aug 2025 (the offseason) were his peak. A good story matters more than the calendar.

## 6. Comments don't drive reach

- His top 20% of Shorts get *fewer* comments per 1K views than his bottom 20%: 0.06 vs 0.21 in 2026.
- Likes per 1K views are the same for both groups.
- The reason is that hits bring in casual viewers.

**New rule:** don't chase comment counts. The levers are the topic and the first 2 seconds. The argument ending is there so people rewatch and share.
