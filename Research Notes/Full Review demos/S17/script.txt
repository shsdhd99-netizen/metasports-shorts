Goalkeepers who were subbed on just for the penalties. Back in {2014|twenty fourteen}, the Netherlands played Costa Rica in a World Cup quarter-final. A hundred and twenty minutes, nil-nil. Then, in the final minute of extra time, Louis van Gaal did something that stunned the whole stadium. He took off his goalkeeper, Jasper Cillessen, and brought on Tim Krul, just for the shootout. Cillessen hadn't been told. We said nothing to Jasper, Van Gaal admitted. And here's the strange part. At Newcastle, Krul had saved just two of twenty penalties. But Van Gaal said he had a better reach, and they had studied every Costa Rica taker. So Krul walked up to each one and told them he knew where they were going. He saved two, diving low to his left both times. The Netherlands won four-three, and Krul was a national hero. Four days later, they met Argentina in the semi-final. Nil-nil again. Penalties again. But this time, Van Gaal had already used all three of his substitutes. He couldn't bring Krul on. Cillessen stayed in goal, and didn't save a single penalty. Argentina won. The one time Van Gaal needed his trick again, he had no substitutes left.
