## 2. Rules in force (digest, Nov 5, 2026). The full text and evidence live in the files named in brackets; when two disagree, `MetaSports - Playbook.md` "THE RULES" wins, then this page's Nov 4-5 additions.

### A. Owner rules (never relax)
- Every output is a DRAFT; the owner posts. Never open YouTube Studio; scheduling only through `Tools/yt_schedule.py` guard rails for a Short the owner approved. [CLAUDE.md, Playbook]
- Never put keys in chat, docs or git (`Tools/.env`, cloud environment only). Never sign in for the owner, create accounts, or change subscriptions.
- Delete by exact id/path only (the `sp_22da5fb4e8` incident); never by name or glob. Never delete and re-upload a Short; ask for a new cut instead. [AGENTS.md, UPLOAD SCHEDULE]
- No promo, teaser, subscribe outro or call to action; the video stops on the payoff. No cards (1.5 s at most, never in the first 4 s, only when nothing else can show the fact). No dead air (`tighten_voice.py` on every voice). [Playbook]
- Client work (Max Claude): his photo, voice and clips are private, processed locally, git-ignored, never uploaded to new services; every video is a draft until he approves; videos carry an "AI" label; his preview page is a draft deploy until the owner says publish. [Clients/Max Claude/README.md]
- Weekly usage ceiling and the "one builder at a time" claim lines in STATE.md. [AGENTS.md, BUILDER]

### B. What to make (premise first: the first 1-3 seconds and the subject decide a Short)
- **NBA lanes:** about half moments (something you can watch, plus one fact the clip can't show); the rest rotates: family stories, watchable curiosities ("why do players do X"), live star record chases, same-day news (morning after or not at all), weekly countdown test. **Paused shapes:** rules/"why isn't X allowed", history retold, news explainers, "is X doomed?", pure record counts, lesser-name lists. [Playbook, Topic and Script Reset, Learned Weights]
- **Stranger test:** would someone who does not follow the sport stop for it? Kids, family, giants, spectacle, the top 3-4 names, a scandal about them: yes. Trivia and role-player stories: no. [Watch Study]
- **FC:** a goalkeeper doing something sneaky or odd in a famous shootout is the proven premise (6 of 6 above 10K); each new story needs its own new sourced fact; no sequels of a trick already shown; other shapes are 30% experiments, one variable each. [Soccer Channel/Playbook, Lessons Learned]
- **Gauge every idea** (`idea_gauge.py`) and **copy-check it** (`copy_check.py`) before scripting; reframe flop shapes or drop them; never retell a bigger channel's story. [Idea Gauging]
- **70/30:** 70% of builds from shapes above the channel median, 30% single-variable experiments; a lane with 3 flops in a row is paused until the Sunday review. [Learned Weights]
- **Facts:** every claim word (still/record/first/never/only/most) has a dated source row in notes.md; two sources for numbers; say only what the team or a primary source said; sensitive/health topics only with the official statement. [Playbook §4, Lessons Learned]

### C. Script and voice
- Line 1 is 11 words or fewer, states the strange thing, frame 1 shows it; no bare number first; no time-bound wording ("last night"); turn early and every 8-12 s; end on the payoff or a twist; simple words. NBA moment voice about 22 s or less (20-30 s total); FC story under 59.5 s, about 230-250 words. [Playbook §2, Retention Upgrade, Full Review]
- Voice: Alex via `voice_take.py` (checks the take, retakes sentences); then `tighten_voice.py`; credits about 1 per character, a Short costs 1.2-4K. [cloud-short §3]
- Hook + foreshadow (line 2 says what the ending is about) and the "but/therefore" rhythm are tests, not yet rules. [Growth Study, H]

### D. Picture and editing
- Footage first: secure frame 1 and one proof clip per beat before writing. Official league/team/broadcast sources or archive.org, never another creator's edit; log every clip in `sources.csv` with `verified: yes`. [Playbook §2, cloud-short]
- Frame 1 sharp, lit, big subject; shot 1 at least 2.0 s; at most 2 shots in the first 3 s, 3 in the first 5 s; about 10-15 shots of about 2 s (FC about 1.6-2.2 s); let a play run 3-5 s; replay the key second once before the payoff (`"replay": true`, closer or 0.5x).
- Fill the frame: wide plays at a 3:4 window over blur (`blurfit_aspect 0.75`); full width only for something that must be read whole. No emoji stickers, no decorative SFX (one only for a visible event), no punch-in zooms or freeze frames as decoration, no forced loop.
- **Proof on the footage:** words/numbers over the play with `overlay.py`; circle the broadcast scorebug; **new Nov 5:** counting numbers (`"count"`) and hold the payoff number for the last 3 s; show the opposing/rejecting label or logo on screen at the key beat; a literal picture for every phrase. [Dyson study, Editing Study]
- Real audio on viral moments is an open test (owner, Oct 28). [Playbook §2.11]
- **New Nov 4-5 (advice, not gates; `edit_lint.py`):** do not show the same source range twice (mark one deliberate replay), keep one source file under 35% of the runtime, avoid back-to-back shots from one source within 3 s. Evidence: reuse showed no retention penalty across 13-15 FC Shorts, so this is for variety; test continues (S26 curve Nov 7). [FC Clip Repetition Study]
- **Removed rules (do not use):** "3+ cuts in the first 3 s", "a new image every second", "no shot over 3 s", "evidence cards for every fact", "6+ source clips", "at most 35% from one clip" as a gate. [Rule Changes Applied Oct 26]

### E. Package and posting
- Title: a short statement pointing at the moment, no ALL CAPS, at most one hashtag; description empty; tags fill 490 characters (`tags_fit.py`); pinned comment is the two-sided argument; one cover. [Playbook §3, UPLOAD SCHEDULE]
- Slots: NBA 7-8 AM or about 3:30 PM ET, FC 12 PM; up to 2 per channel per day with at least 6 h apart, never 3; news the morning after or skipped; judge at 72 hours (stayed % first, then views), not next morning.
- Never re-upload; replace nothing already public. Hub asset store is 1 GB and full: shrink with `hub_pack.py`, upload only after the owner deletes by exact id.
- TikTok/Instagram: post the same file; captions from `crosspost.py`; the 60 s+ cut only near 10K followers. [Crosspost Queue]
- Scheduling: `schedule_ready.py` runs 6:30 AM/PM; `--require-approved` (card has `**Approved:** yes`) is the strict option; every skip is logged with its reason. [Nov 5]

### F. QA (a Short is not done until all pass)
`script_lint.py`, `script_cadence.py`, `qa_short.py --full` (must PASS; verify each clip's player/uniform/scorebug/date and mark `verified`), `edit_lint.py`, then LOOK at frame 1, every overlay, the last frame and the contact sheet (`shot_strip.py`). Deflash one-frame flashes (`deflash.py`). Loudness -14 LUFS, true peak under -1 dB, no clipping. [cloud-short, qa_short.py]

### G. Measurement
- Judge on stayed vs swiped (Studio) or the API engaged-ratio stand-in (under 0.50 means likely under 60% stayed), then views at 72 h, then subs per 1K views. n under 5 is a guess.
- Weekly: Monday Studio export for both channels; Sunday review (top 3 / bottom 3, one rule kept, one dropped). `learn.py` writes `Learned Weights.md`; `fc_study.py`, `channel_totals.py` write the study numbers.
- Monetization lines to track: subs, Shorts views per rolling 90 days (public and engaged), 3M and 10M lines; Feb 1, 2027 changes. [Case studies, Monetization Research]

PC timers (analytics 3:20, schedule 6:30, gap-fill 6:45/7:30, resume) do the daily work while the cloud routines are blocked on keys.
