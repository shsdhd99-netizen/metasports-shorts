## Fixes, in order (if this is your page or someone you're helping)
1. **Make more of what hit:** superstar countdowns of something visual. "Ja Morant's top 6 dunks, ranked", "Wemby's most absurd blocks", "LeBron's chase-down blocks, ranked". Keep "No. 1 is coming" teases, and the best at the end.
2. **Hot takes only on stars**, and always with the play that proves the take on screen. The college-player takes average under 1K.
3. **Fill the frame**: crop to vertical on the action; no 16:9 strip over blur for a play.
4. **Change the handle and name.** "user38494616132257" is the default and looks like a throwaway; pick a real name, a profile picture and a one-line bio.
5. **Pin the dunk video**, and reply to its top comments with follow-up videos ("you said Ja's are better: here's his top 6").
6. **Post 1-2 a day, spread out**, not 5-7 in one afternoon.
7. **Watch for reused-content flags:** some clips carry other creators' watermarks ("RRH REPORT", "OOPS"). TikTok can hold back reused content from For You and from rewards. Use broadcast or team footage and cut around other creators' logos.
8. **Length:** keep every video safely over 1 minute (61-65 s) if the goal is Creator Rewards. Several are exactly 60 s, which may fall just short.
