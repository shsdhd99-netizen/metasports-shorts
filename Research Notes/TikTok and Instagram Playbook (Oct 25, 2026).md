# TikTok and Instagram Playbook (Oct 25, 2026)

This covers cross-posting MetaSports (NBA explainers, 20-45 s, AI narration over broadcast/team footage) and MetaSports FC (Ahmed-style soccer stories: a 59 s YouTube cut plus a 61-80 s long cut) to TikTok and Instagram Reels. Neither account exists yet. The owner posts by hand.

**Tiers:**
- **[V]** official or verified: a platform page, a platform statement, or our own measurement.
- **[S]** several credible sources: press reports and data studies that agree. Vendor blogs are marked "(vendor)".
- **[H]** one source, an anecdote, or my own estimate.

---

## What to do (checklist)

1. **TikTok: open a Personal account, not a Business account.** The owner must be 18+ and live in an eligible country (US, UK, DE, FR, JP, KR, MX, BR), with the account registered there. Business accounts are barred from the Creator Rewards Program (CRP), and their in-app sound picker only shows the Commercial Music Library. [V] [Creator Academy: CRP](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program), [US CRP Terms, Jul 20, 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en)
2. **Instagram: switch to a Professional account of the Creator type (not Business) and keep it public.** Creator accounts get the full music library. Business accounts get a smaller royalty-free set. Public professional accounts can also be indexed by Google. [S] [Buffer (vendor)](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-account-type/), [Kapwing (vendor)](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/instagram-posts-in-google-search-what-creators-need-to-know/)
3. **Use the same name and avatar as on YouTube.** Put the keyword in the username (e.g. @metasports, @metasportsfc). The TikTok bio (160 characters) should say what you post. **Put no phone number or email in the TikTok bio**: the CRP lists contact info in the profile as an "Advertising" ineligibility. You can change the username once every 30 days and the name once every 7 days. [V] [Personalize profile](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/how-to-personalize-profile), [CRP FAQ](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program)
4. **Upload our clean master MP4 to each platform.** Never re-upload a file downloaded from TikTok or YouTube. Another app's watermark makes a video ineligible for TikTok's For You feed and keeps a Reel out of Instagram recommendations. Our own logo is fine. [V] [TikTok Originality Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/tiktok-originality-policy), [Mosseri via Social Media Today, Oct 23, 2024](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-that-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/)
5. **FC: post the 61-80 s long cut on TikTok and Instagram, and keep the 59 s cut for YouTube.** CRP only pays for videos of 1 minute or more. In a 2026 study of 8M brand TikToks, 60-90 s videos had about 3x the median views of 30-60 s videos. Make the cut **at least 62 s**. Two official pages say "over 1 minute" and one says "at least 1 minute", so the extra second avoids the dispute. [V] [CRP eligibility](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/monetization-creativity-program-video-eligible) + [S] [Socialinsider (vendor study)](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/how-long-are-tiktok-videos/)
6. **NBA: keep 20-45 s as the default, and test a 62-70 s TikTok cut on 5-10 stories.** Under 1 minute the video can never earn CRP money. On Instagram, Reels of 45-60 s had the most views; under 30 s did worst. [S] [Socialinsider IG (vendor study)](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)
7. **AI labels:**
   - **TikTok: no label is needed** for a generic narrator voice. TikTok says disclosure isn't needed for "generic text-to-speech (TTS) narration, when the TTS isn't a recognizable voice of a known individual". Never clone a real commentator's or player's voice.
   - **Instagram: turn on the "AI label" toggle.** Meta requires a label for "realistic-sounding audio" that was digitally generated, and "may apply penalties" if you don't.
   - [V] [TikTok AI label](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/ai-generated-content-label), [Meta, Feb 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/labeling-ai-generated-images-on-facebook-instagram-and-threads/)
8. **Keep words and numbers inside the shared safe box: x 120-888 above y=840, and x 120-780 from y=840 down to y=1248.** For TikTok/IG exports, cap caption cards at about 760 px wide and never place the caption centre below y=1185 (62%). The full table and our numbers are in section 5. [V] for TikTok's template, which I measured; [V] for Meta's percentages
9. **Show the title or the context in the first 3 seconds.** The FC format already does this: the title is spoken as line 1. TikTok says to hook within 5 seconds and to cut every pause. [V] [High-quality checklist](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/high-quality-checklist-rewards)
10. **Make videos findable in search.** Say the searchable phrase (player or club + topic) in spoken line 1, show it on the cover, and put it in the first line of the description. No keyword stuffing or tiny hidden text: TikTok can flag that as spam. Search value raises CRP pay. [V] [Search myths and facts](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/Getting-discovered-with-Search)
11. **Hashtags: 3-4 on TikTok (TikTok's own advice). Instagram allows at most 5** (a hard cap since Dec 2025). Hashtags help sorting, not reach. [V] [TikTok checklist](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/high-quality-checklist-rewards), [Instagram @creators via Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)
12. **Post about once a day per account.** In Buffer's data, 2-5 posts a week beat one post a week by 17% in views per post, and 11+ a week beat it by 34%. Start with US midday and evening slots (ET), then move to your own analytics after about 3 weeks. Best-time studies contradict each other. [S] [Buffer frequency (vendor study)](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/)
13. **Copyright hygiene:**
    - Never use the original broadcast audio.
    - Keep broadcast shots short and under narration.
    - Mix in our own stat cards, stills and stock footage. TikTok says voiceover should be "the majority of your video".
    - No live-game footage and no full highlight packages.
    - Repeat infringers are banned on TikTok, and a TikTok copyright appeal sends your contact details to the claimant.
    - [V] [TikTok commentary tips](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/Elevate-your-Commentary-content-with-these-4-tips), [TikTok Copyright Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/copyright-policy/en)
14. **Upload to TikTok from TikTok Studio on the web.** It accepts full 1080p, has "Content check lite" (a pre-check for For You feed eligibility), and runs a music-copyright pre-check. The CRP extra reward needs 1080p or higher. For music, use either nothing or a track from the in-app library; the sound-effect licences are unknown (see Rules Audit). [V] [Content check lite](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/content-check-lite), [Repurpose long videos](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/tips-how-to-repurpose-long-videos)
15. **Instagram Trial Reels: once the toggle appears, use it to test a hook or a length on non-followers.** Instagram can auto-share a trial to followers if it does well in its first 72 hours. [V] for how it works, [Instagram, Dec 10, 2024](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels); [H] for the follower threshold (reports conflict: 200 vs 1,000)

## 1. TikTok Creator Rewards Program (CRP) in 2026

### Who can join [V]
Sources: [Creator Academy: CRP](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program) and the [US CRP Terms, last update Jul 20, 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en). In the US, the program is now run by **TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC**.

| Rule | Exact requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | 18+ |
| Region | You must live in, and have the account registered in, the US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico or Brazil |
| Followers | **10,000 "authentic" followers** |
| Views | **100,000 "authentic" video views in the last 30 days** before you apply |
| Account type | **Personal only.** Business, Organization and political/government accounts are excluded |
| Standing | Account in good standing; a PayPal (or other approved) payment account in your own name; tax forms done |
| How to apply | Profile, then ☰, then TikTok Studio, then Creator Rewards Program. The answer comes within 3 days |

### Which videos earn [V]
- **Length:**
  - The Creator Academy says "original, high-quality content **over 1 minute**".
  - The US Terms say "a duration of **at least 1 minute**".
  - So make it ≥62 s.
- At least **1,000 For You feed views**.
- Not a Duet, Stitch or Photo Mode post. Not an ad, paid promotion or sponsored post.
- A video you delete or make private is dropped and its rewards are not paid.
- **What counts as a "qualified view":**
  - It is watched for ≥5 s.
  - It is unique per account.
  - It is not marked "Not interested".
  - It is not paid.
  - It comes from the 8 CRP countries.
  - **A view from Search counts only after ≥30 s.**
  - Source: [Creator Academy: CRP FAQ](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program).
  - For us, the country rule matters most: views from outside the 8 countries pay nothing, and soccer draws a lot of them. The dashboard splits qualified from ineligible views, so check this early. [H]

### How the pay is calculated [V]
Sources: [RPM: four key factors](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/RPM-understanding-the-four-key-factors), [Additional Reward](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/crp-unlock-additional-reward), [Audience commercial value](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/crp-audience-commerical-value)

- **Standard Reward:** based on qualified views. It varies with where the viewers are and with the video's language.
- **Additional Reward** (since Spring 2025): a "quality multiplier". TikTok scores it on:
  - **average watch time;**
  - **retention after 30, 40, 50, 60 and 90 s;**
  - **completion rate;**
  - **search value**, meaning how often the video is found through Search.
- **The video must also be:**
  - "well-crafted";
  - "engaging";
  - "specialized" (expert in a niche);
  - **1080p or higher.**
- **Audience value:** older viewers and content in the viewers' local language pay more. TikTok says this outright.
- **Payment:**
  - Paid by the 15th of the following month.
  - On termination, TikTok pays amounts above a **$50 minimum**.
  - TikTok "reserves the right to impose a monthly or annual cap" on rewards.
  - All of this is [V] from the US Terms.

### RPM (pay per 1,000 qualified views)
TikTok publishes no rate.

- **What creators report:** about **$0.40-$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views** in the US. Some guides go a little higher; older Creativity-beta figures were $0.20-$0.40. [S] (vendor blogs; e.g. [Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io/blog/tiktok/tiktok-pay-for-1-million-views), [Podcastle](https://podcastle.ai/blog/how-much-does-tiktok-pay-per-view))
- **The old Creator Fund** paid about $0.02-$0.04. Ignore any guide that quotes those numbers for 2026.

### What disqualifies a video or the account [V]
Source: [Creator Academy: CRP FAQ](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program)

- **Anything that isn't eligible for the For You feed is also not eligible for CRP:**
  - **Unoriginal:** "copied from others or has minimal original input or edits".
  - **Low quality:** split screens, meaningless reactions, low-quality images, slide videos.
  - **Advertising:** contact info in the profile.
  - **Clickbait:** content made mainly to farm follows or clicks.
  - Disturbing or inappropriate content.
  - Artificial engagement.
- **The [Originality Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/tiktok-originality-policy) lists as unoriginal:**
  - content copied completely;
  - content "largely repurposed from another source without adding any creative edits";
  - content "combined from multiple sources with little or no additional information";
  - content "with someone else's visible watermark or superimposed logo".
  - The Community Guidelines add "clips that show someone else's watermark or logo". Broadcaster logos and scorebugs count as someone else's logo.
- **Thresholds:**
  - 5 video violations in 30 days makes the account ineligible.
  - **5 account violations in total makes you permanently ineligible.**
  - "5 or more ineligible videos in the past 30 days" can disqualify the account. Source: [Video eligibility](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/monetization-creativity-program-video-eligible).
  - You can appeal an ineligible video within **80 days** and an account disqualification within **30 days**. An account appeal takes up to 14 days to process.
- **Copyrighted clips:**
  - Videos must follow TikTok's Copyright Policy.
  - The creator is "solely responsible for... ownership and originality... copyrights".
  - TikTok can "mute, remove or disqualify" videos with unauthorized music.
  - TikTok's sports pages say "always use official rights-cleared footage". Sources: [US CRP Terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en), [Creating sports content](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creating-sports-content).
  - **How strictly TikTok enforces this on transformed commentary is not published.** [H]
- **AI:**
  - The CRP pages don't mention AI at all.
  - The **TikTok Creator Growth Challenge terms** (Sep 24, 2026) say "AI-generated content (AIGC) is not original content" and that "videos produced primarily by artificial intelligence will not count". That rule is for that program, not CRP, but it shows where TikTok is heading. [V] [Growth Challenge terms](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tiktok-creator-follower-challenge-program-terms-conditions/en)
  - Our videos use real footage, a human-chosen edit and an AI voice. I'd call that "AI-assisted", not "primarily AI", but this is a judgment call. [H]
  - If CRP becomes the main goal, recording the owner's own voice for the TikTok long cuts would remove the doubt. [H]

### Realistic path for a new account
1. **Months 0-2: grow, don't earn.**
   - Post daily on both accounts and lead with proven YouTube winners.
   - The 10,000-follower gate is the hard part. The 100,000-views-in-30-days gate is easy once anything takes off.
   - Our YouTube follow rates are 0.44 subs per 1,000 views for NBA and about 6 per 1,000 for FC (n=1). If TikTok is similar, **10K followers needs roughly 2-20M views.** FC's story format converts far better than NBA explainers. [H, our numbers]
2. **Before you qualify, make every TikTok long cut eligible anyway:**
   - ≥62 s and 1080p.
   - No other platform's watermark.
   - Narration over the whole video.
   - Licensed or no music.
   - Then old videos keep earning after you join. Rewards are per video, from posts that are public after you enrol. Whether views earned before enrolment count is **not documented**. [H]
3. **After joining:**
   - Watch the "ineligible views" breakdown and the per-video RPM.
   - Appeal any video wrongly flagged as unoriginal within 80 days. Attach editing proof: script, timeline screenshot, cut list. TikTok says production photos help.
4. **Expected money:** 500K qualified views a month × $0.40-$1.00 is about **$200-$500 a month**. That's the order of size to expect. [H math on [S] rates]
5. **Not open to us yet: the TikTok Creator Growth Challenge (US).**
   - It needs a YouTube channel with **>10,000 subscribers**. We have about 570 and 29.
   - It pays a $250 welcome reward plus $3,000-$30,000 for follower growth within 45 days.
   - It excludes AIGC and takes an **exclusive, perpetual licence** to the videos you enter. Read it carefully if we ever qualify. [V]
6. **Instagram pays almost nothing per view in the US.**
   - Reels Play ended in 2023.
   - Today's bonuses are invite-only and show up in the Professional Dashboard. Facebook Content Monetization is invite-only too. [S] (vendor: [Fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/instagram-reels-bonus-program-explained), [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/monetize-facebook-reels/))
   - **Treat Instagram as reach and brand-deal value.** Don't spend extra edit time on IG-only versions.

## 2. AI rules: labels, auto-labels, and reach

### TikTok [V]
- **The rule:**
  - You must label AI or heavily edited content that shows **realistic-looking people or scenes**.
  - You also must label when "AI-generated audio mimics the voice of a real person".
  - No label is needed for colour correction, cropping, artistic styles, or **"generic text-to-speech (TTS) narration, when the TTS isn't a recognizable voice of a known individual"**.
  - Sources: [AI label](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/ai-generated-content-label), [Community Guidelines: Integrity & Authenticity](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity).
  - **So our ElevenLabs narrator over real footage needs no label on TikTok.** That holds for the library voice "Alex", the designed narrator, and the clone of our old narration, as long as none of them is a known person's voice.
- **Effect of the label:**
  - TikTok says using the label "won't affect the distribution of your video" and has no impact on organic engagement.
  - A label can't be removed after posting.
  - Unlabelled realistic AI "may be removed, restricted, or labeled by our team".
- **Auto-labels:**
  - TikTok reads **C2PA Content Credentials** and auto-labels AI content made elsewhere.
  - It is testing invisible watermarks. Over 1.3 billion videos have been labelled.
  - Since Nov 2025 it has been testing a **"Manage topics" control that lets viewers see less AIGC**. A video that gets an AI label could lose some viewers who turned that dial down. This is one more reason not to self-label when the rules don't require it. Source: [TikTok Newsroom, Nov 19, 2025](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/more-ways-to-spot-shape-and-understand-ai-content?lang=en).
- **Our files:**
  - I scanned **408 voice MP3s and 14 final MP4s in this repo**. None carries a C2PA/JUMBF manifest. The one byte match was inside an 893 MB source clip and is noise. [V, own check]
  - ElevenLabs says it embeds an invisible audio watermark in its output ([ElevenLabs help](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/audio-detector/what-is-watermarking-and-why-is-eleven-labs-using-it.md)). **No source says TikTok or Meta reads it.** [H]

### Instagram / Meta [V]
- **The rule:**
  - Meta requires people to use its label tool "when they post organic content with a photorealistic video or **realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created or altered**".
  - It "may apply penalties if they fail to do so".
  - Source: [Meta, Feb 6, 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/labeling-ai-generated-images-on-facebook-instagram-and-threads/). Instagram's help page says the same; it was seen in the search index because the page itself is script-rendered. [Instagram help](https://help.instagram.com/761121959519495)
  - **Read literally, a realistic AI narrator is covered.** So turn on Instagram's AI label (Advanced settings, "Add AI label") for every Reel with the AI voice. [V for the rule; H for how Meta treats narration]
- **Effect of the label:**
  - Meta calls labels informational. Since Jul 2024 they read "AI info" instead of "Made with AI".
  - Since Sep 2024, content that was only *edited* with AI shows the label in the post's menu rather than on the post.
  - Source: [Meta, Apr 2024 + updates](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/).
  - Meta has published **no reach penalty** for labelled content. [V that none is published; H on actual effect]
- **Detection:** In Feb 2024 Meta said it **can't yet detect** AI signals from other companies' audio and video tools at scale. So a missing label on narration is unlikely to be caught automatically. That is no reason to skip it.
- **Mosseri on AI** (Jan 2026): he expects AI media to become indistinguishable from real media. He wants to "label AI-generated content and verify authentic content", and he values real-person credibility. He gave no ranking rule. [S] ([Tubefilter, Jan 6, 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/01/06/adam-mosseri-instagram-warning-generative-ai-deepfakes/))

### Do AI-voiced faceless accounts get penalised?
- **TikTok:** nothing official, beyond:
  - originality, where AIGC is "not original" in the Growth Challenge terms only;
  - the viewer AIGC slider, which only matters if the video is labelled.
  - Creators report unexplained "unoriginal" CRP flags. [H] ([Ballerina Alert](https://balleralert.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-disqualification/), anecdotal)
- **Instagram:** nothing official on AI voices.
  - The real penalty is **originality**. Accounts that "primarily post unoriginal content" lose recommendations to non-followers.
  - Instagram's guidelines say a voiceover can make third-party material original. **Our narration plus graphics is the thing that makes the clips "ours".** [V] [Original content guidelines](https://creators.instagram.com/original-content-guidelines/)

## 3. Copyright: NBA and soccer broadcast clips on TikTok/Instagram

### Platform rules [V]
- **TikTok:**
  - IP-infringing content is removed.
  - A **repeat-infringer policy bans accounts**, "immediately" for severe cases, and TikTok can refuse the person a new account.
  - If you appeal a removal, TikTok "will forward your entire appeal to the original reporter, including any contact information", and the claimant "may use this information to file a lawsuit".
  - Source: [TikTok Copyright Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/copyright-policy/en).
- **TikTok music:**
  - Unlicensed music gets the clip **muted, either fully or just the part with the music**. Your voice and effects keep playing.
  - Fix it with "Change sound" or appeal. The web uploader has a music-copyright pre-check toggle.
  - TikTok says that for videos of 1 minute or longer you should "always be sure to use licensed music or original audio".
  - Sources: [Muted video](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/appeal-muted-video), [Longer video strategy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/longer-video-content-strategy).
- **TikTok's own sports guidance:**
  - "Don't just repost clips... include your original commentary."
  - "Always use official rights-cleared footage and tag all relevant profiles or organizations."
  - "Your dialogue or voiceover should make up the majority of your video."
  - Sources: [Creating sports content](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creating-sports-content), [Sports commentary](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/sports-commentary), [Commentary tips](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/Elevate-your-Commentary-content-with-these-4-tips).
- **Instagram/Meta:**
  - Meta removes a reported item "promptly" when the report is "complete and valid". Source: [Meta IP transparency](https://transparency.meta.com/reports/intellectual-property/).
  - Instagram's Terms let it disable accounts that "repeatedly infringe". [S] ([summary of the Terms](https://www.epgdlaw.com/what-do-i-do-if-my-instagram-account-has-been-disabled))
  - Instagram says its originality guidelines "are separate from" IP rules: original ≠ licensed. [V]
- **Mute vs remove:**
  - Music is usually muted.
  - A video owner's report removes the post, and repeat removals threaten the account.
  - Unlike YouTube Content ID, **neither platform lets a sports rights owner simply claim the money and leave the video up**, at least not in any way the public can see. [S]
  - The YouTube "claimed Short over 1 minute is blocked worldwide" rule **does not exist on TikTok/IG**. That is why the 61-80 s cut is possible there.

### How the leagues police it
- **NBA:**
  - A multi-year global partnership with TikTok was signed on Jul 16, 2026. It covers creator access, highlights distribution, GamePlan and "Tap to Watch". **Only "select" creators get access, and there is no public application.** [V] [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-nba-and-wnba-announce-global-partnership?lang=en)
  - Net Influencer reports that "hundreds of creators" got highlight access for 2026-27, and that Tap to Watch links appear on posts mentioning #NBA. [H] ([Net Influencer, Oct 21, 2026](https://www.netinfluencer.com/nba-expands-creator-access-tiktok-tap-to-watch-links-ahead-of-2026-27-season/))
  - The NBA is generally seen as tolerant of short clips with commentary. But broadcasters (ESPN/ABC, NBC, Amazon) own most game feeds, and any of them can file takedowns. [H]
- **Soccer:**
  - The Premier League has a long record of aggressive takedowns (Vine/Twitter era, a lawsuit against YouTube). Live matches are the most policed.
  - I found **no current public creator policy** from the PL or FIFA for TikTok/IG. [S] for history ([SportBusiness](https://media.sportbusiness.com/news/premier-league-settles-youtube-copyright-case/)), [H] for today.
- **What commentary channels report:**
  - Clips with real commentary mostly survive.
  - Raw highlights get removed, and "unoriginal" CRP flags arrive without warning.
  - [H] (creator anecdotes; [FindClout (vendor)](https://findclout.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-reposted-clips))

### Safest practices
1. **Narration from 0.0 s to the end; never the broadcast audio** (commentary, crowd music or arena PA). This also avoids music mutes. [V]
2. **Keep each broadcast shot short (our 1.1-2.1 s cut rhythm) and use it as proof for a line, not as the show itself.** [S]
3. **Add pixels of our own:** stat cards, number overlays, evidence screenshots, maps, stills and stock (Mixkit). TikTok asks for "4+ high-quality visuals" and says to "change your scene at least every 5 seconds". [V] [Common mistakes](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/avoid-common-creation-mistakes), [Storytelling for mods](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/driving-views-with-modification)
4. **No live-game footage and no full highlight packages.** One play per beat. [S]
5. **Network logos and scorebugs:**
   - Pick crops where the 9:16 punch-in leaves them out. TikTok treats "someone else's watermark or logo" as unoriginal.
   - **Don't blur or paint out a logo on purpose.** Deliberately stripping ownership marks from a work can create its own legal exposure (US DMCA §1202). [V for TikTok's rule; H for the legal point, not legal advice]
6. **No blurred letterbox bars.** TikTok says blurred bars are "the giveaway that the video came from another app". Fill the 9:16 frame. [V] [Fill the screen](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/fill-the-screen)
7. **Log every mute or removal** in the Daily Log.
   - Stop using a source (a broadcaster, a league) after its first removal on a platform.
   - Appeal only when the case is clear, because TikTok passes your contact details to the claimant. [V]
8. **Keep footage sources and the edit timeline** for every Short. They are proof of originality for CRP appeals. [V that TikTok accepts editing proof]

## 4. What performs on TikTok and Reels (2025-2026)

### Length
- **TikTok:**
  - TikTok says users spend **50% of their time on longer content**, and that watch time on 1 min+ content has been 5x higher than on short videos. [V] (TikTok's own claim, [RPM page](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/RPM-understanding-the-four-key-factors))
  - Socialinsider analysed 8.0M brand TikToks (Jan-Jun 2026). Median views: **15-30 s = 1,000; 30-60 s = 2,200; 60-90 s = 7,200; 90-120 s = 9,620**. Engagement rate is flat at 4.2-6.0%. [S] (vendor study, brand accounts, correlation only)
  - Buffer found earlier that videos over 60 s got 43.2% more reach (1.1M TikToks). [S] ([Buffer (vendor)](https://buffer.com/resources/longer-tiktoks-get-more-views-data/))
  - **Takeaway:** on TikTok the FC 61-80 s cut is the main product. NBA long cuts are worth testing.
- **Instagram:**
  - Reels can be up to 3 min (Jan 2025).
  - Reels over 3 min are not shown to non-followers. [S]
  - Socialinsider analysed 9.77M brand Reels (Jan-Jun 2026). **45-60 s had the most median views (10,374)**, then 60-90 s (9,790), 30-45 s (8,564) and under 30 s (4,700). [S] (vendor study)
  - Mosseri says there's no single perfect length. The goal is watch time.

### What the algorithm rewards
- **TikTok For You feed:**
  - watch time ("our recommendation system prioritizes longer watch times");
  - likes, shares, comments and skips;
  - variety (it replaces videos too similar to each other);
  - creators in your region.
  - [V] [How the recommendation system works](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/guidelines-recommendation-system-intro)
- **Instagram:** Mosseri says "the top three signals... are **watch time, likes and sends**". Likes matter slightly more with followers. **Sends (DM shares) matter more for reaching non-followers.** [V] (Mosseri, Jan 22, 2025, via [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/))

### Hook, first frame, cover, text
- **TikTok:**
  - Show "a title or clear context within the first three seconds". No greetings or slow intros.
  - Cut every pause.
  - Keep text inside the safe zone.
  - Subtitles help: "over 30% of TikTok users watch videos on mute".
  - [V] [Checklist](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/high-quality-checklist-rewards), [Mistakes](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/avoid-common-creation-mistakes)
- **TikTok covers:**
  - Upload a custom 1080×1920 cover with a short title.
  - Keep the same template every time and keep the text away from the edges.
  - Profiles are a major traffic source.
  - [V] [Video covers](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/video-covers-thumbnails)
  - Profile grids on both apps show roughly the centre 3:4 of the cover (1080×1440), so keep the cover title between about y=240 and y=1680. [S]
- **Instagram:** its 2021 guidance makes Reels "**predominantly covered by text**", blurry or low-res Reels, and Reels with borders or other apps' logos less discoverable. Keep number overlays and captions from taking over the frame. [S] ([Tubefilter, 2021](https://tubefilter.com/2021/02/10/instagram-reels-with-tiktok-watermark-less-discoverable/))
- **Double captions:** leave TikTok's auto-captions off. Our burned-in captions already do the job, and TikTok reads on-screen text and the voiceover for search anyway. [H]

### Search, keywords (TikTok SEO) [V]
- TikTok ranks search results on "relevance... based on things like **video captions, video text, and hashtags**" plus engagement. Source: [Search](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/search).
- It tells creators to use "your video's description, **voiceovers**, captions, and **on-screen text**". Spoken words count.
- **Banned tricks:** stuffing keywords as small on-screen text that you swipe away, or long hashtag lists. These can get a video "flagged for spam".
- Use **Creator Search Insights**, including the "Content gap" tab: searches with few good videos. The "Searches by followers" view needs 1,000 followers. Sources: [Discovered with Search](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/Getting-discovered-with-Search), [Search traffic](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/driving-video-traffic-search).
- **For us:**
  - FC: the spoken title line, the cover title and the first description line should all carry the same plain phrase, e.g. "Goalkeeper hid a cheat sheet in his sock".
  - NBA: name + number + event, e.g. "SGA 20-point streak ended on purpose".
- **Instagram:** public professional-account posts have been indexable by Google since Jul 10, 2025. Write captions in plain searchable words. [S]

### Hashtags
- **TikTok:** "Three to four targeted hashtags is often enough — more doesn't mean better." [V]
- **Instagram:** hard cap of **5**, announced Dec 2025 by @creators. Mosseri has said hashtags don't raise reach. [V/S]
- **Suggested sets:**
  - NBA: `#nba #basketball #[team] #[player]`
  - FC: `#football #soccer #[club or competition] #[player]`
  - The NBA–TikTok tie-in may add "Tap to Watch" to #NBA posts. [H]

### Posting times and frequency (US audience)
- **Frequency:** Buffer analysed 11.4M TikTok posts from 150K accounts (Oct 2025). Compared with posting once a week: **2-5 a week gave +17% views per post; 6-10 gave +29%; 11+ gave +34%.** More posts mostly raise the odds of a breakout. Buffer's baseline is 2-5 a week. [S] (vendor study)
- **Best times:** the studies conflict.
  - **TikTok (Buffer, 7.1M posts, Sep 2026, local time):** Sunday 9 a.m., Monday 1 p.m., Sunday 1 p.m.; Saturday is the best day. [S] (vendor study)
  - **TikTok (Sprout Social):** Tue-Thu 2-6 p.m. [H] (seen only in secondary summaries)
  - **Instagram (Buffer, 9.6M posts, Sep 2026):** best days Wed, Thu, Tue. For Reels it suggests trying 6-11 p.m. [S] (vendor study)
- **Plan:**
  - 1 post a day per account.
  - Weekdays: midday ET (matches our 11:00/12:00 ET YouTube slots), or 6-9 p.m. ET for Instagram.
  - Weekends: mornings.
  - After 3 weeks, switch to each app's "followers active" chart. [H]

### Cross-posting the same file
- **Fine** on both apps if the file is ours and has no other platform's watermark. Ahmed's network posts the same file to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. [V, our Story Format Study]
- **Instagram's originality ranking:**
  - Eligible content "has no visible watermarks". Source: [Instagram, Apr 30, 2024](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/recommendations-and-originality).
  - Since **Apr 30, 2026** the rule covers photos and carousels too. Accounts that "primarily post unoriginal content" are "no longer shown in places where we recommend content". [S] ([MediaPost, May 1, 2026](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/414773/instagram-algorithm-update-discourages-reposted-co.html))
  - Eligibility returns when **most posts in a rolling 30 days are original**. Check **Account Status**. [V] [Original content guidelines](https://creators.instagram.com/original-content-guidelines/)
  - Their test: "if someone could remove your contribution and the content would be virtually the same", it needs more of you. Our narration and graphics pass it. Plain clip montages would not.

### Instagram Trial Reels
- **How they work** [V] ([Instagram, Dec 10, 2024](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)):
  - A trial Reel is shown to **non-followers first**. It isn't in followers' Feed or Reels tab and stays off the grid.
  - Metrics (views, likes, comments, shares) arrive after about 24 h.
  - You can share it to everyone at any time, or let Instagram **auto-share it if it does well "based on the views it receives within the first 72 hours"**.
- **Who gets it:** professional, public accounts that are eligible for recommendations. Reported follower minimums conflict (200 for professional and 1,000 for personal, or 1,000 for everyone). Check in the app. [H]
- **How to use it:**
  1. A/B test the hook. FC: spoken title vs. a "Back in [year]" opener. NBA: scene vs. number opener.
  2. Test length: the NBA 25 s cut vs. a 45 s cut.
  3. Post the winner normally, or let it auto-share. Archive the loser.
  4. Never post near-duplicates as regular Reels.
  5. While the account has almost no followers every Reel reaches non-followers anyway, so trials matter most once there's a following to protect.

## 6. Account setup for a faceless sports account

### TikTok
- **Account type: Personal.**
  - **Business accounts can't join CRP** (US Terms §1f; Creator Academy).
  - In-app sound search on a Business account shows **only the Commercial Music Library**. Trending sounds are off-limits. [V]
  - Business accounts add things like a link-in-bio and contact buttons, which we don't need.
  - "Creator account" is no longer a separate type; the Personal account is the creator account. [V] [Business vs Personal](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/TikTok-Business-vs-Personal)
- **Username:** use a keyword handle and avoid random strings. You can change it once every 30 days, and the profile link changes with it. [V]
- **Name:** "MetaSports | NBA explained" / "MetaSports FC | Football stories". The name can change once every 7 days. [V]
- **Bio (160 characters):**
  - Say what you post and why it's worth it. Example: "The NBA stories nobody explains. New one daily."
  - No email or phone: the CRP "Advertising" rule. TikTok's profile guide says to add an email, which contradicts it, so leave it out until a brand deal needs it.
  - Adding a website link needs **1,000 followers**. [V]
- **Profile picture:** the same as YouTube, so people recognise it across platforms. [V]
- **First posts:**
  1. Start with 3-5 proven YouTube winners, re-cut to TikTok rules: long cut, safe-zone captions, no other app's watermark.
  2. Then 1 a day.
  3. Make a playlist per series once the feature is available.
  4. Don't delete underperformers: deleted or private videos lose CRP eligibility.
  5. Never buy followers or views. CRP counts only "authentic" followers and views.
  6. [V/S]
- **Posting tools:**
  - TikTok Studio web: 1080p uploads, Content check lite, music pre-check. [V]
  - Bulk upload needs 1,000+ followers.
  - Scheduling: one Creator Academy page says it needs **10,000+ followers**; another says anyone can schedule up to 30 days ahead. It's moot while the owner posts by hand. [V, conflicting]

### Instagram
- **Account type: Professional, Creator category, public.**
  - Creator accounts get the full music library and creator insights.
  - Business accounts have a reduced, royalty-free library. [S] (vendor)
  - Public is needed for Trial Reels and for Google indexing. [S]
- **Profile:**
  - Same handle and avatar as TikTok and YouTube.
  - Put the keyword in the Name field ("MetaSports | NBA stories"), because people search by name.
  - Bio limit: 150 characters. [S]
- **Per-Reel settings:**
  - AI label ON (because of the AI voice).
  - Custom cover with the title inside the middle 3:4.
  - ≤5 hashtags.
  - "Upload at highest quality" ON in the app's media-quality settings. [S]
- **Watch Account Status.** It shows whether the account is eligible for recommendations under the originality rules. [V]
