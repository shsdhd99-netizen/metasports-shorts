# Deep study: "Football clubs that are the best at scouting players" (xeysfc, FhOw7eZ3Ivk), Nov 6, 2026
Owner sent it as "a great video to deep research". Sources: the channel's Shorts list (yt-dlp, no cookies) and a Gemini watch report (`Watch Reports/FhOw7eZ3Ivk.json`).
Tiers: [V] measured (views); [S] Gemini's reading or a single example; [H] untested idea for us.

## Why it matters [V]
- **1.1M views on a channel whose median is 1,200** (48 Shorts; next best 10K). About **900x its own normal**: the premise did this, not the channel's audience.
- **The title is only hashtags** ("#football #footballshorts #relatablememes"). It got no help from a title, so the video itself carried it. This matches our finding that titles barely matter.

## What it is [S]
- **76 s, 31 shots (2.5 s average), AI male voice at about 165 wpm, quiet hip-hop bed from 0 s, no sound effects, 2-word white captions in the middle.**
- **Line 1 is the promise, in plain words, also on screen:** "Football clubs that are the BEST at SCOUTING players!" Frame 1 is football action, not a face.
- **The story:**
  - one scout (Sven Mislintat) spots an unknown striker in a league "nobody cared about";
  - every top club passes;
  - Dortmund pays €4.5M for Lewandowski;
  - even its own fans ask "who did we just buy?";
  - then Dembele for 15M, sold for 125M;
  - **payoff: a rapid-fire price list of today's megastars**: Aubameyang 13, Sancho 8, Bellingham 23, Haaland 20.
- **Editing devices:**
  - a name tag the moment a new person appears ("Sven Mislintat", "Michael Zorc");
  - archive clips framed inside a retro CRT TV (makes old 4:3 footage look like a style, not low quality);
  - side-by-side photo graphics (scout + player);
  - **reaction-meme inserts at the beats where the viewer would react** (Obama, Travolta looking confused at "who did we just buy?", IShowSpeed);
  - the final frame is the biggest name (Haaland).
- **Accuracy:** several lines look wrong or loose (the dates and who scouted Bellingham and Haaland). It went viral anyway; **we don't copy that**. Every number of ours needs a source.

## Why it worked (our reading) [S]
1. **A money gap anyone gets in a second:** bought for almost nothing, became a superstar or was sold for a fortune. It's our "number gap" lane; our own "news number gap, superstar" Short did 23.7K [V].
2. **Famous names stacked faster and faster toward the end.** The climax is a list, so the last 5 s pay off over and over. That lines up with our finding that hits keep viewers to the end (0.88 vs 0.65 audience at 90% of the runtime) [S].
3. **An underdog + "everyone passed" + "versus their own"** (their own fans doubted it): the same shapes that win for Ahmed's Goal (rejection ladder) and our FC data.
4. **A "clubs that are the best at X" frame** turns one story into a category. The viewer expects more examples, so they stay.
