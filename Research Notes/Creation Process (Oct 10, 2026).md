## The gate

A premise dies if any answer is no.

1. **The clip.** Name the moving shot for second 0. The spoken fact and the jersey agree. A building, a runway still, an empty floor, a presser, or the wrong action is a no.
2. **The promise.** The first eight words make one claim. The last line pays that claim with a new fact. A why-title that the script cannot answer is a no. A last line that asks a question the video never answered is a no. Flagg ended on a question and was watched to the end at 19K. Do not copy that ending, and do not treat it as proof that a question kills a video.
3. **The lines.** Read the script out loud and delete any sentence that repeats the previous one, lists names to sound big, previews a game that has not been played, or stalls with "the crazy part" before a fact arrives.
4. **The family.** Not the same visible fact as a post from the last seven days. One family a week.
5. **The sources.** Every proper noun and number traces to a page opened that day. A signup wall means the sentence is not locked. Re-check anything time-sensitive the morning it is voiced.
6. **The search.** `copy_check.py` is a lead. An empty FRESH is unknown. On Oct 10 it missed Coach Clank's underhand Short at 10.9 million views. A person reads a raw search, and opens a known giant by URL, before the script is saved into `Productions`.

Length after the gate: 90–110 spoken words, about 22–26 seconds. "But" inside the first 40% of the words. Two escalations after the turn. Six moving clips before any ElevenLabs spend. Music stays off until one future upload tests it alone. The retention winners have no music bed. Sound effects only when that frame shows the sound, catalog volume, none in the first second, never twice, cap five, and zero is allowed.

The last shot has to be the opening clip, and the last sentence has to still be true over that picture. Number 6 can loop a six. A line that says "nobody" cannot sit on a person wearing the thing.

## After each upload

At 48 hours, add one row to `Outcome Log.md` with stayed%, average view, length, and views in separate cells. Then change one rule in this file or in Lessons, and say which upload forced the change. Do not rewrite the process from a video that is under 48 hours old. Do not invent a Reach explanation. The Oct 4 cards are the only feed-share read, about 90–97% from the feed.
