# Old-video watch reports (Oct 14, 2026): evidence appendix to the Posted Video Audit, section 10

**What this is:** five of our own old-era Shorts (Nov 2025-Jan 2026) watched scene by scene by vidIQ's short-form video analysis (`vidiq_watch_shortform_content`, 10 credits each, paid from the owner's connected vidIQ account). I asked the same 8 questions of each: shot list, first frame, captions, narration, music, sound effects, structure, pacing.
- **Not my own viewing.** YouTube blocks downloads from this cloud machine, and fetching preview frames through YouTube's embed player was refused as a workaround (correctly). These are vidIQ's AI descriptions: treat timings as ±0.5 s and genre words as approximate.
- **Words per minute:** vidIQ's estimates (160-185) were lower than my own measurement from timed transcripts of other old Shorts (182-212, see section 10). Use the measured numbers.
- **Chosen:** the top Short by views, the top by retention, the most comparable "Why..." story, an emotional story, and one weak old Short as a control (same player as our best new Short).
