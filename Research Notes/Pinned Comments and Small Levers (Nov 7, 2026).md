# Does a pinned comment matter? And the small things that raise a Short's chances (Nov 7, 2026)
Tiers: [V-official] YouTube's own help pages or staff; [V-ours] measured on our channels (API daily files Nov 6); [S] third-party study or small sample; [H] untested.

## 1. Pinned comments: verdict
**No evidence that a pinned comment changes reach. Keep it only when it costs nothing and does a job.**
- **[V-official] YouTube's ranking list for Shorts doesn't include comments:** "Our systems use the signals for % of viewers who chose to view, avg. view duration and avg. % viewed to inform ranking" (YouTube Help, *Search & discovery tips: Shorts*, support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225).
- **[V-official] Comments aren't a reach switch:** Todd Beaupré (head of recommendations, Creator Insider): turning off comments or ads does not lower reach (summary at outlierkit.com/resources/how-youtube-recommendations-work, linked to the official video rHLjxrbXmmY).
- **[V-ours] Engagement per view doesn't go with views on our channels.** Rank correlation with views, Shorts with 300+ views and 3+ days old:

| Metric | NBA (n=39) | FC (n=15) |
|---|---|---|
| Likes per view | -0.10 | -0.35 |
| Shares per view | -0.04 | -0.17 |
| Subs per view | +0.25 | +0.14 |
| **Engaged ratio** (stand-in for "chose to view") | **+0.33** | **+0.47** |
| **Avg % viewed** | **+0.30** | **+0.62** |

(Comment counts aren't in the files; likes are the nearest engagement stand-in. Correlation, small n.)
- **[S] Vendor claims** that pins lift replies "up to 30%" are about replies, not reach. Our Round 6/7 reviews found the same: comments don't drive reach.
- **What a pin is still good for:**
  - (a) the source line ("Source: AP recap, Nov 6"), which cuts "fake" replies and builds trust;
  - (b) pointing to Part 2 of a series;
  - (c) correcting a mistake fast.
  The API can't pin, so it's a 10-second manual step. **Don't spend effort writing debate questions.**

## 2. Small things that do move a Short (in order of evidence)
1. **Frame 1 and line 1 decide "chose to view"** [V-official + V-ours]. It's YouTube's first-listed signal. On our NBA channel, Shorts with 74%+ stayed have a median of 10.4K views vs 2.9K. Frame 1 shows the promise, big and moving; line 1 is 11 words or fewer.
2. **Replays count as views** [V-official: since 31 March 2025 a Shorts view counts every time it starts or replays]. A clean loop (the last frame flows into the first; an unfinished last line into line 1) adds views directly, and raises avg % viewed.
3. **Keep it short** [V-ours, small n]: NBA Shorts of 25 s or less had a median of 5.6K vs 2.8K for longer ones (same stayed %, so likely loops). FC stories stay 50-59 s; their avg % viewed matters most (+0.62).
4. **Hold the payoff to the end** [V-ours, 20 curves]: hits keep 0.88 of the audience at 90% of the runtime vs 0.65 for flops. Payoff in the last 2-3 s, held on screen.
5. **Pace 230-260 wpm** [V-ours, small n]: above 230 wpm did better on both stayed and views.
6. **Fill the frame** [S]: big, cropped vertical action. Never a 16:9 strip over blur for a play (the TikTok page review, our edit rules).
7. **A searchable title** [V-official + S]: search is one of the places Shorts are ranked ("how well the metadata matches the viewer's search"), and a Shorts filter in search arrived in 2026. Put the player's name and the key word in the title. Wording beyond that is a small factor (528-Short competitor study).
8. **Hashtags: 0-1** [S: Metricool's 2026 study of 799K videos says hashtags drove under 1% of views; Shorts with hashtags got fewer views but more engagement]. Our rule (0-1, #football or none) stays.
9. **Related video link** [V-official feature]: in Studio, link each Short to its Part 2 or a related Short. It sends viewers on to our next video; it isn't a reach lever. Worth it for series.
10. **Don't delete or re-upload a slow Short** [V-official]: the first hour is mostly regular viewers, and YouTube keeps testing a video for weeks. Re-uploads also risk duplicate flags.
11. **Honest hooks** [V-official]: satisfaction surveys and "Not interested" taps feed ranking. A hook the video doesn't pay off costs future reach.
12. **Trending sounds** [V-official, mixed]: YouTube says they can put a Short on that sound's page and "increase discoverability", but they're "not essential". Under a narration it's a small, optional extra. Test once with a quiet Audio Library track.
13. **Posting time** [S]: small (Round 6). Keep the grid; don't chase hours.

## 3. What changes for us
- Keep the pinned comment as a one-line source note, or "Part 2" pointer. Drop the "debate question" pin from the upload template's must-haves.
- Add **related video** to the series posts (Lendeborg Part 2, the "most missed" lists, the goalkeeper series). It's an owner step in Studio, since we don't open Studio.
- Everything else is already in the edit rules. The two cheapest wins to check on every draft: **the loop frame** and **the held payoff**.
