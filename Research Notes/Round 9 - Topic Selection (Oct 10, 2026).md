## The gate before any new premise

All five have to pass. A copy-check FRESH does not skip any of them.

1. Name the clip. Second 0 is a moving person. The fact is on that person, or the first eight words are one contradiction while a person plays. A building, a runway still, an empty floor, or the wrong action fails.
2. Family calendar. Not the same visible fact as a post from the last seven days.
3. Saturation is confirmed by a person, not by an empty tool row. Open a known giant by URL when one exists.
4. Write stayed%, average view against length, and views in three separate sentences when judging an old post. Do not collapse them.
5. Lock the payoff sentence to a page opened that day. A signup wall means the sentence is not locked.
