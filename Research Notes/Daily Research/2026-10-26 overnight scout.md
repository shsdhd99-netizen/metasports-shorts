## #1 Bones Hyland's fake-throw technical

**Facts and sources**
- Wolves beat Toronto 117-94 on Sunday, Oct 25. With under two minutes left, Hyland was called for a foul, "grabbed the ball and cocked his arm back like he was going to throw it at the official". Referee Pat Fraher said something, Hyland "with a huge smile, cocked his arm back again", and Fraher called a technical. Fraher "was only briefly annoyed… the pair immediately went back to having a good-natured conversation while the Raptors shot the technical free throw." Source: [Yahoo Sports / The Big Lead](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/bones-hyland-shocked-technical-foul-132004283.html).
- Wolves up 25 with under two minutes left; "Apparently only one of the two was in on the joke." Source: [Heavy](https://heavy.com/sports/nba/minnesota-timberwolves/timberwolves-bones-hyland-punished-ref-making-joke/).
