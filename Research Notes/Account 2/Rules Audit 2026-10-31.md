## B. The "never post / never open Studio" rule: split by safety vs cost

**What is safety (keep):** (1) no agent publishes, schedules, edits or deletes a public video; (2) no key leaves the environment; (3) no cookies on yt-dlp; (4) no login to the owner's Google account through a browser.

**What is cost, not safety:** the owner does every manual step: (a) typing the title, tags, pinned comment from `UPLOAD.md`; (b) uploading the file; (c) choosing the version (music/no-music, real audio/narrated); (d) logging the version posted (this is why the A/B tests are unreadable); (e) the Monday Studio export; (f) setting "made for kids / altered content" flags, thumbnail (Shorts: cover frame). Every one of those is a chance for the wrong file to go up, and (d) and (e) are already broken.

**What could be safely automated without changing any public video** (no public change, so within the spirit of the rule if the owner allows it):
1. **Upload as PRIVATE (or unlisted)** through the YouTube Data API: the owner opens Studio only to flip Private to Public. Needs a Data API upload scope on the existing OAuth (the current analytics scope is read-only; this has not been checked) and, importantly, the owner's explicit approval, because it creates an object on the owner's channel. It does not change any public video. Risk: a private upload that someone flips by accident; mitigated by naming ("DRAFT ... do not publish"). **Needs the owner's yes. Not recommended without it.**
2. **A paste-ready package** the main account already writes: UPLOAD.md. Make it one file per Short with the exact title, tags, comment, version-to-post and filename; add a QR or copy button on the Hub card. No permission change.
3. **Auto-fill the post log**: instead of asking the owner which version was posted, read the posted video's duration and title with the existing public yt-dlp metadata check (no cookies) and match to the draft (22.0 s vs 23.6 s). This fixes the music A/B at zero risk.
4. **Auto-export of analytics**: `yt_analytics.py` already runs daily; extend it to pull the Studio-style fields (it already pulls retention curves). The "stayed to watch" gap may be filled by the API metric `engagedViews` / `shortsViewsShownVsStayedToWatch` if available; check, don't assume. If it is, the Monday export is no longer needed.
5. **Alerts**: a daily message to the owner "Short X is due to post at 7:30; here is the file and the text" via the existing Hub/phone to-do (no new access).

**Recommendation:** keep rule 1 (no publishing) verbatim; reword "never open YouTube Studio" to "never use the owner's browser session or cookies"; ask the owner once whether items 1 and 4 are acceptable. Everything else is zero-risk.

## C. Rules that hurt most (ordered by my estimate of damage, all [S]/[H])

1. **"Same-day news or not at all" and the fixed posting-time windows (together):** n=1 evidence for the first, none for the second, but they force owner timing and discard Shorts. Relax to a 24-36 h window and let the owner post when convenient.
2. **Hard NBA length window 20-30 s:** our own data shows length does not predict views (-0.13), and the winners exist at 10-53 s. Costs time cutting good lines. Replace with a story-length target and test 20-22 vs 27-29 s.
3. **"Global names only" for soccer:** the only evidence is another channel (Ahmed); our three FC winners are trick/object stories, and big names (Zidane, Maradona, Coutinho) did not carry non-trick stories. Cuts the idea pool.
4. **Not logging the posted version because the owner hand-posts and nothing records it:** not a rule, a gap that ruins the music test, the real-audio test and the post-hour test. Fix with automation item 3 above.
5. **The owner doing every upload step by hand (cost of rule 1b):** risks wrong-file posts and missing data; weigh against the safety benefit with the private-upload option.
6. **Delete-sources-after-post:** removes the ability to re-cut a Short that is doing well or fix a claim. Move deletion to after the 72 h read.
7. **"Not a question" for line 1/title:** the little data we have leans slightly the other way; untested for the spoken opener.

## D. Rules to keep no matter what

- Branch separation: Account 2 pushes only to `account2`, never force-pushes, never edits the main account's files.
- No promo/follow outro, no cards: the owner's call and our data agrees (the evidence is [S], but harm risk of removing is nil).
