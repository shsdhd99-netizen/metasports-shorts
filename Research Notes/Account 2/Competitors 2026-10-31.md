## Lesson

**One lesson (4 of the top 5 support it, all [S]):** the biggest outliers open with ONE line of text (or one spoken rule) in the first frame and carry the whole Short on a single clear moment, with no cards and no follow ask. That matches our rules; it is not a reason to change format. Caveat: 4 of 5 are voiceless clip/compilation channels (reposts), a different lane from our narrated stories, and Gemini's 'music' calls are unreliable, so do NOT copy 'no voice' or 'loud music'.
