## 2. Repeatable patterns (tier and n)
1. **Voice-over story Shorts start talking at 0.0 s with the premise as a plain sentence.** 8/8 narrated competitor videos (rows 1-5, 8, 9 plus Wemby facts excepted) start the voice at or within 0.3 s; and so do all three of ours. We already match. SUPPORTED, n=7. Not a differentiator.
2. **The CoachClank-style caption is small, calm and lowercase/sentence-case, white, 3-4 words, mid-lower centre, no colour, no box.** CoachClank 2/2 and Ahmed 1/1 identical. Bleacher Report's explainer uses colour on keywords and a top headline; Courtside uses only a rank label. PROVEN that this is what CoachClank and Ahmed do (n=3); HYPOTHESIS that it helps. We are the outlier: big ALL CAPS red/green.
3. **Pacing is slower than ours.** Story channels run 1.9-2.8 s average shots (CoachClank 2.0 and 2.8, Ahmed 1.9, BR 2.6, Rebound 3.2) with 0-2 cuts in the first 3 s. Ours: 0.96-1.3 s and 2-5 cuts in 3 s. PROVEN (measured, n=5 vs 3). Whether slower wins is HYPOTHESIS: the competitors are also 60-120 s videos, ours 23-28 s, so cuts per second may reflect length strategy. CoachClank's 15-31 s YouTube winners were not watched.
4. **Pure footage, zero cards in CoachClank (0 of ~52 shots) and Rebound/Courtside; cards appear in Ahmed (stills, screenshots on grey) and BR (stat cards).** Ours: 1-3 cards per Short. SUPPORTED, n=7.
5. **Real audio/sound-bites are the content in clip channels (HoH, NBA, BR clips, GD) and a mid-roll device in Rebound Rewind (press-conference faces, 3 clips).** CoachClank's two story Shorts have none. Our Bronny Short's Luka clip follows pattern 5. SUPPORTED, n=7.
6. **Fast narration is typical:** CoachClank 265 and 309 wpm, Ahmed 274 vs ours 221-239. PROVEN n=3; we are 15-25% slower than CoachClank. Not known whether speed itself drives retention.
7. **A persistent premise label is used by Rebound Rewind (title burned in for all 67 s) and BR (top headline first 3 s), not by CoachClank.** Matches our approved `--premise-line` test. SUPPORTED, n=2.
8. **Endings are factual closers, not questions** (0 of 9 narrated end on a question; ours also none). Not a pattern we can learn from.
9. **Payoff placement:** the three CoachClank/Ahmed stories pay off in the last 5 s with a named scene ("clapping for Moody"). Ours do too (record maths, "shot might protect his spot"). No gap.
