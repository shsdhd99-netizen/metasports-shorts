# Niche study: soccer story Shorts (Oct 24, 2026)

**Owner's ask:** evidence on what makes soccer story Shorts go viral, the biggest channels in the lane, a deeper read of the Ahmed's Goal data, and a hard look at our current rules.

**What I used:**
- **Ahmed's Goal CSV:** 1,365 Shorts with views as of Oct 21, plus the Basketball (1,175) and Business (614) CSVs, used to check whether a finding holds on the other two channels.
- **Fresh YouTube channel listings (Oct 24)** for 30+ soccer channels: subscribers, and views on their last 60 Shorts. RSS feeds gave upload times.
- **33 TikTok copies** of the Ahmed network's Shorts, read from their public pages: runtime, plays, sound type, AI label and the auto transcript. They cover 13 Goal, 10 Basketball and 10 Business Shorts; 30 of them were posted Oct 8-24, 2026.
- **Our own Studio export** (Oct 17-24), web sources, and earlier notes in this repo.

**Evidence tiers:**
- **[V]** verified: I measured it in data.
- **[S]** several credible sources agree.
- **[H]** a single source, a small sample, or my reading.

**Limits:**
- After about an hour, YouTube blocked single-video pages from this server ("Sign in to confirm you're not a bot"). So for channels other than Ahmed's I have titles and views, but not runtime, voice or music.
- Reddit and X could not be reached.
- I found no interview with Ahmed at all (section 6).

## Top 7 findings

### 1. The story format runs 70-80 s. Our under-60 s rule protects us from copyright claims; it is not part of the format.
- **What:**
  - Every Ahmed Short I could measure runs over a minute.
  - YouTube's one hard rule is about copyright claims, not length as such.
  - Our own three Shorts show length did not decide the swipe.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Ahmed's runtimes:** all 33 network Shorts I could measure on TikTok (30 of them from October 2026) run **61-101 s** (median 77 s). Ahmed's Goal's ten October posts run 63-93 s (median 71.5 s). The earlier 73-Short YouTube sample gave a minimum of 58 s and a median of 76 s. [V]
  - **YouTube's rule:** "Any Short that is over one minute in duration with an active copyright claim of any type" is blocked worldwide, and is "not playable or recommended" until the claim is resolved ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877)). [V official]
  - **TikTok:** Creator Rewards only pays on videos over 1 minute ([Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/how-to-join-the-tiktok-creator-rewards-program), [Makeinfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-how-organic-monetization-works-in-2026)). That probably explains why Ahmed never goes under 60 s. [S]
  - **Our own Shorts:**
    - Average time watched: S01 (60 s) 56 s, S02 (64 s) 47 s, S03 (71 s) **50 s**.
    - "Stayed to watch" is decided in the first second or two, before length can matter. S02, the shortest of the two longer ones, had the worst score (56%). [V]
- **Tier:** [V] for the facts; [H] for how length itself affects views. Nobody has tested that cleanly.
- **What we should do:**
  - Keep the YouTube cut under 60 s whenever it uses archive broadcasts.
  - Make a **65-75 s long cut** of the same script (one more escalation beat) for TikTok and Instagram, if the owner approves cross-posting.
  - Try a long cut on YouTube for about 1 Short in 5, but only when the footage is low-risk: photos, our own graphics, labelled recreations, stock.
  - Check Studio's copyright column within an hour of posting. If a long Short gets claimed, remove the claimed part (YouTube's own suggested fix) or re-post the sub-60 cut.

### 2. The strongest title signal is conflict with your own side: betrayed, refused, rejected, sued, "their own".
- **What:** titles where a player or club turns on its own team, country, sponsor or teammates sit far above the channel median. The effect holds after allowing for when each Short was posted, and it shows up on all three Ahmed channels.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Ahmed's Goal:** 1,146 format-era Shorts, Feb 2025 to Oct 2026, leaving out the 15 newest.
    - Conflict words (own, betray, refus, reject, sued, enemies, hated, didn't like, disrespect, revenge, regret): **median 610K vs 313K** (n=87 vs 1,059). That is 1.63x the median of the 100 Shorts posted around each one.
    - "Their own" alone: **781K, 2.28x** (n=35).
    - Conflict titles make up 13% of the top quartile and 4% of the bottom quartile.
  - **The same test on the other channels:** Basketball 1.31x (n=103), Business 1.25x (n=85). [V]
  - **Examples:**
    - "Players who refused to play in matches" (4.7M)
    - "World Cup players who got rejected by their own country but then got revenge!" (4.6M)
    - "Enemies who became teammates then enemies again" (4.7M)
    - "Teams that got sued by their own sponsor" (4.1M)
    - "Players who sued their own team" (2.8M, then 1.5M as a repeat)
- **Tier:** [V]. Themes come from title keywords, so they measure the title promise, not the script.
- **What we should do:**
  - Add a "versus their own" lane to the Idea Bank: a player against his club, country, coach, sponsor or teammates.
  - Hook with the strong verb (refused, sued, betrayed, rejected).
  - S01's "secret object" hook also works. This is a second proven lane, not a replacement.

### 3. Two of our own data claims don't hold up on Ahmed's numbers.
- **What:**
  - (a) "Plural 'Players who...' titles beat singular ones (318K vs 217K)" cannot be reproduced, and the comparison is not possible on his data.
  - (b) "Category words such as 'trouble' lose" is only weakly true.
- **Evidence for (a):**
  - In the format era (Feb 2025 on) there are **no** singular "The player who..." titles. Across the whole CSV I found 4, all from Mar-Nov 2024.
  - The 51 titles that start with He/This/The/They have a median of 14K, but **50 of the 51 date from Mar-Nov 2024**, when the channel was tiny. That is a timing effect, not a title effect.
  - Titles starting with a plural noun sit exactly at the channel median (0.99x Goal, 1.05x Basketball, 1.05x Business). [V]
- **Evidence for (b):**
  - Vague words (crazy, insane, wild, drama, trouble): 290K vs 323K on Goal (0.87x, n=92), 0.80x on Basketball (n=77), 1.23x on Business (n=31).
  - On Goal they appear equally often in the top and bottom quartiles (10% vs 11%).
  - "Trouble" itself: n=3, **median 1.0M**, including the 5.9M "Football players who got in trouble for the silliest reasons".
  - Strong verbs do lift: 412K, 1.35x (n=107). [V]
- **Tier:** [V].
- **What we should do:**
  - Delete the plural-vs-singular claim from the Lessons and the skill. Title shape is neutral; post the spoken line 1.
  - Change "category words lose" to: **say the stakes and say them specifically**. A category word is fine with a sharp qualifier ("...for the silliest reasons").
  - S02's flop is better explained by frame 1 (a face, not the fire) than by the word "trouble".

### 4. Ahmed's own format is fading, and he has already changed it.
- **What:**
  - His medians have fallen through 2026.
  - He posts about half as often as before.
  - Most of his recent Shorts are news questions.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Ahmed's Goal by period:**

    | Period | Median views |
    |---|---|
    | Feb-Jun 2025 | 411K |
    | Jan-May 2026 | 357K |
    | Jun-Jul 2026 | 228K |
    | Aug-Oct 2026 | 218K |
    | Last 60 Shorts (listing of Oct 24) | 178.5K |

  - **Channel totals:** vidIQ shows 20.5M channel views in the last 28 days ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@AhmedsGoal/)).
  - **Uploads:** RSS shows 15 uploads in 14 days (about 1.1 a day, down from about 2). Basketball is at 0.7 a day.
  - **Business:** its median fell from 326K in its first month to about 36K. [V]
  - **News questions:** 59% of Goal titles in Aug-Oct 2026 are questions ("Why did Cristiano Ronaldo get fined exactly only two hundred fifty five euros by Portugal", "Is De Zerbi about to get sacked soon?"). That compares with 0-5% before June 2026.
    - They do as well as his evergreen Shorts (median 238K vs 245K, n=65 vs 45).
    - They include his biggest recent hits: "What exactly happened between Cristiano Ronaldo and Portugal?" 1.9M, "Where should the final of the 2030 World Cup be played" 1.3M, "How the heck did Emiliano Martinez just end up at Chelsea" 1.2M. [V]
  - **Format details also changed:**
    - ALL-CAPS titles fell from 78% (Jul 2025-May 2026) to 4-5% (Jun-Oct 2026).
    - "Episode N" labels stopped after 2025.
    - "Back in [year]" opens line 2 in only 1 of his 30 October scripts (section 2). [V]
- **Tier:** [V]. Newer Shorts have had less time to collect views, so the last month reads a little low.
- **What we should do:**
  - "Change nothing, copy Ahmed" now means copying a target that keeps moving. Copy the mechanics that still hold (the title as line 1, one story, turns, a payoff, the edit) and stop treating his 2025 habits as rules.
  - Test a **topical lane**: a story Short hung on this week's news ("Why did X just...?" answered with a story from the past). Make about 1 in 4 posts topical for the next 20 posts.

### 5. Even Ahmed's format needed about 100 Shorts before it took off. Three Shorts can't settle a rule.
- **What:** Ahmed's channel sat at about 17K a Short for two months after switching to the new title style, then jumped. Our S01 spike and its quick fade is a normal first test wave.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Ahmed's Goal by phase:**

    | Phase | Shorts | Median views |
    |---|---|---|
    | Before the switch (Mar-Nov 2024) | 93 | 14K |
    | First new-style Shorts (Dec 2024-Jan 2025, approx.) | 106 | 17K |
    | The next run | 66 | 340K |
    | Feb-Jun 2025 | 268 | 411K |

    Dates are interpolated from upload counts. [V]
  - **Our channel's daily views:** 2,084 on Oct 21, 28,963 on Oct 22 (the S01 spike), 1,893 on Oct 23 (Studio Totals). [V]
- **Tier:** [V] for the numbers. Why Ahmed took off in Feb 2025 is unknown [H].
- **What we should do:**
  - Judge a rule on 20-30 Shorts, not 3.
  - Keep 1 a day.
  - Log every variable on every post, so the next 20 posts can answer questions. The log should cover:
    - music on/off;
    - length;
    - what frame 1 shows;
    - the hook type (object, conflict or news);
    - whether a star is named.

### 6. A numbered serial about one global star is the biggest lever we don't use.
- **What:** the one other English channel doing narrated soccer story Shorts at scale that I found (FOOTY FACTS) took off with numbered parts of one star's life.
- **Evidence:**
  - **FOOTY FACTS** (241K subscribers):
    - Its 761 Indonesian-language Shorts have a median of 34K.
    - Its recent English run of 29 Shorts has a median of **309K**.
    - Inside that run, "Zlatan Part 1-9" has a median of **1.4M** (n=9), against 162K for its one-off stories (n=20).
    - Biggest: "The Wild Journey of Zlatan Ibrahimovic at Ajax! Part 2" 4.9M, and "The Chaotic Story of Balotelli vs. Mourinho at Inter Milan!" 6.8M. [V]
  - **Ahmed's series:**
    - He reuses a winning title as a series. 103 titles were used more than once in the format era.
    - First posts: median 433K (1.20x). Later posts: 330K (1.01x). So a repeat holds the channel median even though it rarely beats the original. [V]
  - **Stars:**
    - Ahmed rarely names stars in titles. When he does (Messi or Ronaldo, n=11) it is 1.46x, but most of those are news questions.
    - The same week's Goal posts with mid-tier names (Grant Holt, David James, Jica Popescu, Gilberto Silva, Chaka Heslop) got 29-67K. Ronaldo's €255 fine got 515K and Rio Ferdinand's missed drug test 180K. [V, small n]
    - Stars don't save a weak opening, though: our S02 had Balotelli and kept only 56%. [V]
- **Tier:** [V] for the counts; [S] that the serial causes the lift (one channel).
- **What we should do:**
  - Test one serial: "[Star]: Part 1" to "Part 5", one club or one season per part, each a full story with its own payoff. Zlatan, Balotelli, Cantona and Mourinho fit.
  - Keep "a famous name within 10 s" as the default. Allow niche stories only when the hook is absurd and concrete. Ahmed's Bayern 27-0 friendly against an amateur side has 8.1M TikTok plays.

### 7. Voice and music aren't where these Shorts win or lose, and we haven't logged ours.
- **What:** I found no evidence that an AI voice hurts in this niche, and no evidence either way on a music bed. We can't learn from our own Shorts because the version posted was never recorded.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Ahmed's voice:** the voice type is unknown. He has never said, and none of his 33 TikTok copies carries an AI label (that label is mostly self-declared, so it proves little). [V]
  - **CoachClank (NBA)** uses a fast AI voice. 0 of 180 of its top comments mention the voice (`MetaSports - Playbook.md`). [V, adjacent niche]
  - **Studies on AI voices are mixed.** One found human narration gave more enjoyment, imagery and recall in stories ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2451958826001429)). Another found generic AI voices rated *more* trustworthy than human ones ([PLOS ONE](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0332692)). A third found voice quality mattered more than human vs AI ([TechSmith](https://www.techsmith.com/blog/ai-voices-avatars-in-training-videos/)). [S]
  - **Music:** studies of narrated video find music changes mood but not retention ([Twente thesis](https://essay.utwente.nl/fileshare/file/87899/Peters_MA_faculty.pdf), [Journal of Educators Online](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1393962.pdf)). Ahmed's TikTok sound is "original sound", with the bed mixed in (the earlier study measured a -45 dB floor). [S]
  - **Our own Shorts:** the Outcome Log says "Version (music / no music): not recorded" for S01-S03. [V]
  - **YouTube's monetisation policy** targets "templated storylines" and "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates", not AI voices as such. Channels with "too much" of it can't monetise ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), [TechCrunch Jul 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/)). [V official]
- **Tier:** [S] that an AI voice is fine for views; [H] for music.
- **What we should do:**
  - Keep Alex.
  - Add one first-person aside per script ("Honestly, I think..."). Ahmed does this, and it makes each Short less like a template.
  - Keep the bed, but alternate music and no-music by day for the next 20 posts and log which went up.

## The five specific questions

1. **Does under 60 s hurt a format whose median is 70-76 s?**
   - Unknown. Nobody has tested it cleanly, and our n=3 can't tell.
   - What is known: the story loses one escalation beat (about 45-60 words at Alex's pace). Fewer seconds usually means a higher % viewed. And "stayed to watch" is settled before length matters.
   - The real costs of going short are TikTok (no pay under 1 minute) and maybe depth. The real cost of going long is a claimed Short being blocked worldwide.
   - Answer: keep YouTube cuts under 60 s while we use broadcast footage, and test long cuts where the footage is safe. [V facts / H effect]
2. **Is a music bed better or worse?**
   - No evidence either way for short-form.
   - Studies on narrated video find mood effects but no retention effect. Ahmed uses a bed.
   - It costs nothing if the bed is licence-clean (ours is generated).
   - Answer: keep it, log it, alternate it. [H]
3. **Does an AI voice hurt in this niche?**
   - No sign of it. CoachClank (NBA, AI voice): 0 of 180 top comments mention the voice. Research is mixed.
   - Ahmed's own voice type is unknown.
   - The policy risk is "templated" content at channel level, not the voice.
   - Answer: keep Alex and make each script clearly researched and our own. [S]
4. **Is the title-as-line-1 formula still working in 2026?**
   - Yes, as the standard for this lane: every one of Ahmed's 33 October scripts uses it, and our S01 kept 80%.
   - But the channel built on it is losing reach. Its best recent Shorts are news questions read as line 1 ("What exactly happened between Cristiano Ronaldo and Portugal?").
   - The formula is not the bottleneck. Frame 1 and the topic are. [V]
5. **Global stars vs niche stories?**
   - Stars help: FOOTY FACTS' Zlatan serial; Ronaldo news at 515K against 29-67K for mid-tier names in the same week.
   - But a star does not rescue a weak frame 1 (S02, Balotelli, 56%).
   - The bigger title lever is conflict or stakes (1.6x).
   - Answer: a famous name by default, the conflict in the hook, and niche only with an absurd concrete fact. [S]

## Big things we are not doing

1. **A topical lane.** Ahmed's 2026 pivot to news questions did as well as his evergreen Shorts, and gave his biggest recent hits (finding 4). We make only evergreen stories.
2. **Numbered serials** about one star (finding 6).
3. **Cross-posting** to TikTok and Instagram. Ahmed posts the same file on all three (TikTok: 391K followers, 64.7M likes). TikTok pays only on videos over 1 minute, which argues for a long cut.
4. **YouTube auto-dubbing.** Since Feb 2026 it is open to all creators, in 27 languages, with 6M+ daily viewers ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-auto-dubbing-tops-6m-daily-viewers-expands-to-27-languages), [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/youtube-expands-auto-dubbing-to-27-languages-adds-more-natural-and-expressive-voices-88923.htm)). Ahmed has it on. The owner can check the setting in Studio. [S]
   - YouTube also showed many Portuguese, Italian and Turkish channels' titles to us in English, so foreign-language channels already compete in English feeds. [H]
5. **Logging the variables** we keep arguing about (music, frame 1, hook type, star or not). Without it, the next 20 posts can't settle anything.
6. **Likes.** Our like rate is 2.6% (S01: 771 likes on 30,099 views) and 1.9-2.0% (S02/S03). Ahmed's Goal's median is about 5.2% on YouTube, and his TikTok copies get 6-15% (n=13). The cause is unknown. Conflict and emotional stakes are the obvious candidates; track it per post. [V numbers / H cause]

- **Analysis set:** the 1,146 Shorts from the takeoff (about Feb 2025) to Oct 2026, leaving out the 15 newest.

### Story types he repeats because they work
- **103 titles were posted more than once.** Their first posts had a median of 433K (1.20x). The 232 repeats had 330K (1.01x). Titles used only once: 0.97x.
- So he repeats his winners, and a repeat lands at the median rather than repeating the hit. [V]
- **Repeated families include:**
  - rejected-then-proved-wrong;
  - sued their own team;
  - craziest ways players got scouted, cut or dropped;
  - teams that almost won the Champions League;
  - facts that sound fake but are 100% real;
  - iconic shirt numbers;
  - players who refused.

### The plural vs singular check
- **The singular group barely exists.** A search for a singular noun followed by "who/that" finds 6 titles. Only 4 are real singular titles ("The only player who could stop Messi", "The man that Barcelona signed instead of Ronaldo"...), and all 4 are from Mar-Nov 2024; the other 2 are a plural title and a question. Titles starting He/She/This/The/They: 51 (median 14K), 50 of them from Mar-Nov 2024.
- **Plural titles are just average.** Titles starting with a plural noun are 0.99x on Goal (n=516), 1.05x on Basketball (n=469) and 1.05x on Business (n=368).
- **Verdict:** the 318K vs 217K figure is not supported. Ahmed's data has nothing to say about singular titles. [V]
