## The look (copy this exactly)

### Captions
- White, sentence case (NOT ALL CAPS, NOT neon green)
- 2–3 words per chunk (sometimes 4), synced to VO
- Centered, mid-frame (over the action, not lower-third TikTok style)
- Soft black shadow OR thin outline — never thick Hormozi stroke, never colored fill
- No title card, no "THE REAL STORY" pill, no big number graphics as the main visual
- assemble.py preset `subtitle` + pixelSize ~62 is in the right family; do not use hormozi / one-word-color

### B-roll grammar
- Picture must illustrate the EXACT words being said that second
- New shot every ~2–4 seconds (one idea per shot)
- Prefer: reaction faces, the named player in that jersey/era, the ball/moment of the story
- Soft blur bars top/bottom OK when cropping 16:9 → 9:16 (Clank does this constantly)
- Scorebugs OK; neon burned social captions NOT OK
- NO empty courtside / random chairs / stock stills that don't match the line
- NO mid-video "infographic card" as the main plate (Clank stays on footage)

### Script DNA (already in playbook)
1. Open on a SCENE / claim / broken rule (not a quiz)
2. Turn with "You see" / "But" in first third
3. End on punchline / consequence / arguable quote — then stop
4. Voice ~250–290 wpm, same AI voice always
5. Length 22–34s for story hits; 90–170 words

### Titles
- 4–7 words, often a question, 1 emoji optional, no hashtag wall, no description

## Why Batch 2 #1 final.mp4 fails the Clank feel

1. **Opener mismatch** — Heat-era dunk while VO says "92% pay cut" / Sixers deal. Clank would open on the signing/Sixers/money moment, not a random Heat poster.
2. **Graphic card mid-cut** — "$52M → $3.9M / THE REAL STORY" slide. Clank does not leave game footage for a designed card.
3. **Word≠picture** — "$4 million" over Heat dribble; "Maxey and Jaylen" over blurry empty NBA chairs. Instant scroll-away.
4. **Roster beat is lazy** — Embiid jog, missing Maxey/Jaylen highlights timed to their names.
5. **No Sixers-era LeBron plate** — story is Philly pay cut; eye needs red/blue Sixers energy or the contract moment, not Cavs/Heat/Lakers montage only.
6. Captions are closer to Clank than your old neon ALL CAPS — captions are not the main problem; **B-roll mapping is**.
