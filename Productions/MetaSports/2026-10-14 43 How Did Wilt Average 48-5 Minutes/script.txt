Games last 48 minutes. Wilt averaged forty-eight and a half. For a whole season. How? Overtime. In 1961-62, his team played {3,890|three thousand, eight hundred ninety} minutes. Wilt played {3,882|three thousand, eight hundred eighty-two} of them. Every second of seventy-nine games. Every overtime too. No rest. No foul-outs. No bench. So where did the missing eight minutes go? January 3rd, 1962. Los Angeles. Just over eight minutes left in a close game. Referee Norm Drucker hit Wilt with two technical fouls. Ejected. The only eight minutes Wilt sat all season, a referee made him sit.
