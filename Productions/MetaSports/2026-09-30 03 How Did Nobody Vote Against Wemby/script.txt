Every Defensive Player of the Year in NBA history had at least one voter who picked somebody else. Until Victor {Wembanyama|wem-bun-YAH-muh}. You see, all 100 voters put him first. The first unanimous winner ever, and at 22, the youngest to ever win it. But that's not even the crazy part. He was a unanimous Rookie of the Year too. And the only other players to ever win both Rookie of the Year and Defensive Player of the Year are Michael Jordan and David Robinson. That's the whole list. Jordan, the Admiral, and a 22-year-old who's still getting better.
