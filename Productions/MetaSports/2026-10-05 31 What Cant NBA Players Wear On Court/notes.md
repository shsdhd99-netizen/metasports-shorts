## Verified claims
- Rulebook (Official 2025-26 NBA Playing Rules, Jan 2026, https://cdn.nba.com/manage/2026/01/Official-2025-26-NBA-Playing-Rules.pdf): Rule 2 Sec II(b) "The officials shall not permit players to play with any type of jewelry."; (e) equipment "designed to increase a player's height or reach ... shall not be used"; Player/Team Conduct and Dress (4) "players must keep their uniform shirts tucked into their pants, and no T-shirts are allowed"; (5) "The only article bearing a commercial 'logo' which can be worn by players is their shoes."
- J.R. Smith, Sep 29 2018: NBA said he could be fined each game he did not cover a Supreme tattoo on his right leg. https://www.si.com/nba/2018/09/29/cavaliers-jr-smith-supreme-tattoo-fine-1 (quote "NBA rules prohibit players from displaying any commercial logos or corporate insignia on their body or in their hair"). He was threatened, not shown fined.
- LaMelo Ball, Nov 17 2023: NBA required him to cover "LF" tattoo below left ear; he covered it to avoid fines (ESPN, Wojnarowski) https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/38922029/nba-requiring-hornets-lamelo-ball-cover-tattoo-says-commercial-logo ; Jan 2024 league stopped threatening fines (Yahoo https://sports.yahoo.com/hornets-guard-lamelo-ball-will-be-allowed-to-play-with-once-banned-tattoo-uncovered-211344491.html).
