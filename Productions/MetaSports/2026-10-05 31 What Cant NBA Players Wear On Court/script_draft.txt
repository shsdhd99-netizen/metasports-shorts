The NBA can fine you for a tattoo. The rulebook says the only commercial logo a player can wear is on his shoes. In 2018 J.R. Smith got a Supreme tattoo on his leg. The league told him cover it or be fined every game. In 2023 LaMelo Ball got the letters L F behind his ear. That's his middle name, LaFrance, and his clothing brand. The NBA made him tape it over. And in 2001 the NBA fined nine players five thousand dollars each, including Shaq and Kobe, for shorts that were too long. Jewelry, untucked jerseys, T-shirts: all banned. The only logo allowed on an NBA player is the one on his shoes.
