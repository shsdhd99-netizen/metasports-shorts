The NBA legend who made up an insult to destroy someone. Back in March {1993|nineteen ninety-three}, a Bullets guard named LaBradford Smith scored {37|thirty-seven} against Michael Jordan's Bulls in Chicago. It was the best game of his career, and Washington still lost. But Jordan claimed that after the game, Smith told him, nice game, Mike. The two teams played again the very next night. So Jordan reportedly told his teammates he'd match Smith's {37|thirty-seven} in the first half alone. By half-time, he had {36|thirty-six}. He finished with {47|forty-seven} in a {25|twenty-five}-point blowout. But here's the wild part. Smith never said it. Jordan later admitted he made the whole thing up.
