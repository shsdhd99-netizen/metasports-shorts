Joel {Embiid|em-BEED} thought he would never dunk again. He said it himself, on Friday. His left knee held him to fifty-seven games over the last two seasons. Then, in the Sixers' last game before the season starts, he faked the handoff, went up, and threw it down. He finished with twenty-seven points, the most of anyone on the floor. So how did he explain the dunk? "I guess it's good to dunk."
