# #30 How Many Steps Can Giannis Legally Take (2026-10-05)
Lane: rule/ban (travel / gather step), Giannis eurostep. Concept: Giannis's "three steps" are legal because the gather does not count. Added value: official rule text read from the rulebook + the 2026-27 emphasis check (traveling NOT on the list).

## Claim -> exact source
| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Rule 10 Section XIII: first step occurs when a foot touches the floor after the gather; two steps after gathering | Official 2025-26 NBA Playing Rules PDF (Jan 2026), https://cdn.nba.com/manage/2026/01/Official-2025-26-NBA-Playing-Rules.pdf : "A player who gathers the ball while progressing may take (1) two steps ... The first step occurs when a foot, or both feet, touch the floor after the player gathers the ball." Rule 4 Sec III defines the gather. |
| Gather foot is not a step | NBA refs' The Huddle EP2 card (https://x.com/OfficialNBARefs/status/1115297799648763904): "The step during the 'gather' of the ball ... is not included as one of the two steps" |
| Refs posted a travel they missed | https://x.com/officialnbarefs/status/1075073263922020352 (Dec 18 2018): "We missed this one - it is a traveling violation." Player not named in the post; Short does not name him. |
| 2019 clarification did not change the rule | https://www.nba.com/news/board-governors-clarification-traveling-official-release |
