Giannis looks like he takes three steps. It's legal. Here's why. The count only starts when he gathers the ball. That landing foot is free. Rule ten, section thirteen: the first step is the next foot to touch the floor after the gather. So it's gather, step one, step two. Three footfalls, zero violations. A fourth is a travel. The league's own referees even posted one they missed. And this season's new referee points of emphasis? Push-offs, screens, straight-line contact. Traveling isn't on the list. So when Giannis drives in Miami, count it. Gather, one, two. Nothing more is legal.
