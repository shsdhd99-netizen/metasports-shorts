**Viewer question in second 1:** "Who is that, and how has a man this tall never played?"

**Re-checked Oct 13 (22:50 UTC):** still on the Raptors training-camp deal. His first preseason chance is vs the Knicks on Oct 13. Preseason doesn't count, so the payoff stays true until he plays a regular-season minute.
**Risk flagged by the researcher (kept on purpose):** Sharp is a niche player, and a transaction story sits next to the weak contract lane. The script stays on ONE measure ("minutes that count"), and the hook leads with Wembanyama's name and the height paradox, not the transactions.
