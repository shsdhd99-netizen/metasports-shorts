A miss from thirty-six feet does not count. But only in the first three quarters. The play has to start in the backcourt. And it has to leave in the last three seconds. If it misses, the miss belongs to the team, not the shooter. If it goes in, it counts as one for one. The league tested that in summer league, then passed it. Players used to wait out the horn, because a late make would not count. Now an early miss cannot hurt him. A fourth-quarter miss still can.
