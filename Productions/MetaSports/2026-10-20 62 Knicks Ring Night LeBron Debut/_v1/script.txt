LeBron James dreamed of finishing his career in Madison Square Garden. Tonight, the Garden chanted this: We don't want you! And the craziest part? The only reason he's not a Knick is that the Knicks won. In June, they beat the Spurs for their first title in 53 years, and LeBron said, "Hey, I can't." His agent said if they hadn't won: There would be no board. He'd be going to the Knicks. So he signed with Philly. And his debut was tonight, in the Garden, on ring night. LeBron had 14, 9 and 7, and the Sixers won, 106 to 100. The Garden didn't want him, so he spoiled their ring night.
