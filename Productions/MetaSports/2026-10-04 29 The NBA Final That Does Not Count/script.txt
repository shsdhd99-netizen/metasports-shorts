Only nine thousand one hundred seats will host an NBA final. But it is not a high school gym. {Hinkle|HINK-ul} Fieldhouse opened in nineteen twenty-eight. It did host the nineteen fifty-four Indiana high school final. The NBA still booked December eleventh there. For three years that final sat in Las Vegas. This year the higher seed keeps the semis at home. Only the championship leaves. It leaves for Butler, in Indianapolis. Nine thousand one hundred people will be in the building. Every other Cup game counts in the standings. This one does not.
