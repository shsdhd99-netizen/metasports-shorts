## Claims → source quotes

- Rule, in one second: the worst record does not get the best odds. NBA.com, June 5, 2026, Jeff Zillgitt: "To incentivize winning, the three teams with the worst records will not have the best odds to win the No. 1 pick. Each of those teams will receive two lottery balls; each of the next seven non-play-in teams will receive three lottery balls." https://www.nba.com/news/what-nba-had-in-mind-with-draft-lottery-reform
- "Draft relegated" and the lost ball. NBA Communications, May 28, 2026: "Teams that do not qualify for the NBA Playoffs or NBA Play-In Tournament will receive three lottery balls each, except that – to provide an increased incentive to win – the teams with the three worst records will be “draft relegated” and lose one of their lottery balls." The system "will take effect beginning with the 2027 NBA Draft" and "will be in effect for the 2027, 2028 and 2029 NBA Drafts." https://pr.nba.com/nba-board-of-governors-approves-new-draft-lottery-system-to-address-tanking
- 5.4% vs 8.1%. Same NBA.com piece: "The teams with the three worst records have a 5.4% chance at the No. 1 pick ... the remaining non-play-in teams have 8.1% odds at the No. 1 pick."
- No back-to-back No. 1. NBA Communications: "No team’s pick will be permitted to be the first pick in two consecutive NBA Drafts or a top-five pick in three consecutive NBA Drafts." This Short does not say the Wizards are already barred. It says the restriction starts with the 2027 drawing.
- Jazz fine, the escalation before the twist. NBA.com, Feb. 13, 2026: "The Utah Jazz organization has been fined $500,000 for conduct detrimental to the league" after the Jazz "removed two of the team’s top players, Lauri Markkanen and Jaren Jackson, Jr., before the beginning of the fourth quarter and did not return them to the game, even though these players were otherwise able to continue to play and the outcomes of the games were thereafter in doubt." https://www.nba.com/news/jazz-pacers-fined
- What the old drawing still produced. NBA.com on the same reform story: the 2026 lottery in Chicago "resulted in the Washington Wizards receiving the No. 1 overall pick." On-screen in the NBA's own clip: "PICK 1 / WASHINGTON WIZARDS." https://x.com/NBA/status/2053558794823581796
