One new rule. The worst NBA record no longer gets the best lottery odds. Miss the playoffs, and the basement used to be the prize. But the three worst records now lose a lottery ball. They get two. The seven teams just above them get three. Five point four percent if you lost the most. Eight point one percent if you won a few more. In February the league fined the Jazz five hundred thousand dollars for pulling two top players. A fine was not the twist. Finish last, and you are draft relegated. Win a few more, and you hold the better ball. Starting in twenty twenty-seven, nobody can be number one two years in a row.
