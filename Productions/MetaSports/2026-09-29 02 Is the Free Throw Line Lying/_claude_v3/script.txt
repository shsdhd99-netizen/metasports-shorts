Every NBA free throw is shot from 15 feet… except it isn't. You see, the rulebook measures that line from the backboard, not from the rim. The rim sits 6 inches off the glass, and it's 18 inches wide, so the middle of the hoop is 15 inches in front of the backboard. Which means every free throw is really 13 feet, 9 inches. But here's the weird part. The three-point line IS measured from the middle of the basket. So the two most important lines in basketball are measured from two different places. And some of the biggest guys in the league still can't make it from 13 feet 9.
