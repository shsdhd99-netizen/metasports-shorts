# #20 Would Bad Boys defense be legal now (2026-10-04)

Lane: rule / "would not be allowed today" (then-vs-now): new 2026-27 Freedom of Movement points of emphasis vs the Bad Boy Pistons.
Concept: Bad Boys' defense versus today's contact rules.
Series: Then vs Now.
Added value: our own comparison of a verified number gap (Detroit allowed 92.9 ppg in the 1989 playoffs vs a 107.9 league average in the 2026 playoffs) with the league's own new rule video, framed as an argument (fouls nobody called, or did the rules kill defense?). Real audio of the league's narration is quoted.

## Numbers and sources (every number read from the source)
- 92.9: Detroit's opponents averaged 92.9 points over 17 games in the 1989 playoffs (Detroit won the title; league average that postseason 104.9). Basketball-Reference, 1989 NBA Playoffs, team opponent per-game table: https://www.basketball-reference.com/playoffs/NBA_1989.html
- 107.9 ("a hundred and eight"): league-average points per team per game in the 2025-26 playoffs (85 games). Basketball-Reference league stats: https://www.basketball-reference.com/leagues/NBA_stats_per_game.html
- Rule: NBA Official posted 2026-27 Points of Emphasis Part 2 on Sept 29, 2026 (Freedom of Movement: Perimeter and Post, Straight-Line Pathway Plays): https://official.nba.com/2026-27-points-of-emphasis/ and the post https://x.com/NBAOfficial/status/2104934389666677091 (post time 2026-09-29 14:00 UTC)
- "Grab a cutter, foul; bump a dribbler off his line, foul": from the narration of that video (grab-and-hold on a cutter = illegal contact; defender contact that moves a dribbler off a straight-line path = foul). Quote used verbatim from the video audio: "the defensive player will make contact that moves him off that path." Summary also in Blazer's Edge (Dave Deckard, Sep 29): https://www.blazersedge.com/nba-news-rumors/116607/nba-highlights-2026-27-points-of-emphasis-for-referees
