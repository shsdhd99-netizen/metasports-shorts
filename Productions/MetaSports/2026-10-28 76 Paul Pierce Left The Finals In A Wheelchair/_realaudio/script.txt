Paul Pierce left the NBA Finals in a wheelchair. Game 1, 2008, against the Lakers. He went down holding his knee, and they wheeled him to the locker room. Minutes later, he jogged back out, scored 11 more points, and Boston won. He was named Finals MVP. Eleven years later, on ESPN, he made a confession. I just had to go to a bathroom. This year, on a podcast, he took it back. Who asked for the wheelchair? Like, why would I take a wheelchair to go use the bathroom?
