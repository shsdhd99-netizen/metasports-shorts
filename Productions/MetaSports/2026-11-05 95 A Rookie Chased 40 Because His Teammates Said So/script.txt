His teammates talked a rookie into chasing forty, and it backfired. Yaxel Lendeborg had thirty-seven points against the Clippers, with Steph Curry sitting out. The Warriors bench started yelling at him to get forty. With the game nearly won, he took a three. Missed. Another three. Missed. A third. Missed. Steve Kerr pulled him after that one. Afterward, Lendeborg said he has never been a stat chaser, and called it peer pressure. He finished on thirty-seven. The three shots he took to get to forty added nothing.
