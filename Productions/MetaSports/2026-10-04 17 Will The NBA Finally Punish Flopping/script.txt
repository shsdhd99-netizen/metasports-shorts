Flopping has been a technical foul in the NBA since twenty twenty-three. But NBC Sports calls the enforcement inconsistent at best. Now the NBA has posted a video of last season's flops, and says refs will call more technicals. The test is STEM: secondary, theatrical, exaggerated movement. But there's a catch. The NBA is also cracking down on push-offs. Thunder coach Mark Daigneault says if defenders know that, they might be just flailing to try to bait those calls. A head snap isn't a flop. Neither is Pascal Siakam sitting down after a jumper. Reacting to contact is legal. Selling it costs a technical foul and a free throw.
