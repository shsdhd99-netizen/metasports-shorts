Flopping has been a rule. The penalty is a technical foul. But not every fall is a flop. The test is secondary, theatrical, exaggerated movement. Refs will call more technicals when a defender exaggerates contact and theatrically falls. Timberwolves coach Chris Finch says just stop rewarding the flopping. The league posted a video of 2025-26 flops. Thunder coach Mark {Daigneault|DAYN-yo} says they might be flailing to try to bait those calls. In that video, {Pascal Siakam|see-AH-kum} sitting down after a jumper is not a flop. A {Deni Avdija|DEH-nee av-DEE-ya} head snap after minimal contact is not a flop.
