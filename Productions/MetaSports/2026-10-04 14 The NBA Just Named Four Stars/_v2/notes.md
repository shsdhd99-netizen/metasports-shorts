## Claim -> source

ESPN page opened: https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50063714/nba-issues-new-guidelines-instructs-refs-crack-push-offs (Ben Golliver, ESPN, Sept 29, 2026).

| Claim | Quote |
|---|---|
| The move is an off-arm push-off | "A push-off most commonly occurs when an offensive player uses his non-dribbling arm to contact a defensive player so that he can create space to attempt a shot from the perimeter." |
| Force is the foul | "referees should assess an offensive foul if the ball handler's off arm contacts the defender with \"force\" or is \"fully extended\" to create an advantage." |
| Jaylen Brown's contact is a foul | NESN, quoting Monty McCutchen in the NBA video: "This contact by Brown is forceful. It is not just firming up, and the offensive foul was correctly called by the slot official." https://nesn.com/boston-celtics/news/ex-celtics-star-jaylen-brown-nba-officiating-change/2907dfcc01c93edacb9d2355 |
| Paul George only felt the defender | Same NESN page, McCutchen: "George does not fully extend his off arm, nor does he push off his legal defender, Sam Hauser, in a forceful manner. Instead, George uses his arm to feel for his opponent and, in doing so, he does not initiate any contact that would be deemed illegal." |
