Bronny's three-point shooting jumped from twenty-eight percent to thirty-nine. So did he actually get better? Supporters say yes. Forty-one percent from the field, up from thirty-one, and nearly three points a night over forty-two games. But critics say look closer. He started just one of those forty-two games, played under nine minutes a night, and his turnovers ticked up, not down. The Lakers did make his deal fully guaranteed. So is thirty-nine percent real growth, or just forty-two small games?
