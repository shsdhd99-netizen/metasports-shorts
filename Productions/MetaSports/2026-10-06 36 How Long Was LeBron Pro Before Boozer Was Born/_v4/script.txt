LeBron's teammate from his rookie year now has a son in the NBA. Carlos Boozer started seventy-five games next to a teenage LeBron in Cleveland. This summer, Memphis drafted his son, Cameron, third overall. And here's how long LeBron has been around: he played in the NBA Finals thirty-four days before Cameron was born. Now Cameron is the youngest player in the league. LeBron, in year twenty-four, is the oldest. Two generations of Boozers. One LeBron.
