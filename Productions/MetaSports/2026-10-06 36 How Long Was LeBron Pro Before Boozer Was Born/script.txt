LeBron played in the Finals before this rookie was born. Cameron Boozer, the Grizzlies' number three pick out of Duke, was born July eighteenth, two thousand seven. LeBron's Finals ended June fourteenth, thirty-four days earlier. He had debuted in two thousand three, three years and eight months before Cameron arrived. Now Cameron is the league's youngest player, one of only ten born in two thousand seven, and LeBron, in year twenty-four, is the oldest. But the strangest part is who LeBron played with back then. Carlos Boozer, Cameron's father, started seventy-five games beside rookie LeBron in Cleveland. Now that teammate's son is the rookie.
