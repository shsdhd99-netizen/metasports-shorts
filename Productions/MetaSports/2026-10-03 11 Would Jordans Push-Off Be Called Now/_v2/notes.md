# notes: #11 Would Jordan's Push-Off Be Called Now (v2)

- Lane: rule / officiating (off-arm push-off)
- Concept: Jordan's 1998 Finals push-off on Bryon Russell, judged under the NBA's 2026-27 Four F's.
- Added value: The ending is the split ESPN already drew. Jordan stays clean. Jaylen Brown's shove does not.

## Claim -> source

Page opened: https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/50063714/nba-issues-new-guidelines-instructs-refs-crack-push-offs (Ben Golliver, ESPN, Sept 29, 2026).

| Claim | Quote |
|---|---|
| No foul was called in 1998 | "No offensive foul was called, Jordan made the shot and the Bulls won their sixth championship of the 1990s." |

| The Four F's | "The \"Four F's\" are feeling, firming, force and full extension." |
| Force or a full extension is an offensive foul | "referees should assess an offensive foul if the ball handler's off arm contacts the defender with \"force\" or is \"fully extended\" to create an advantage." |
| Jaylen Brown shoved Kelly Oubre in the chest | "Jaylen Brown raised his right forearm and shoved into the chest of then-Philadelphia 76ers forward Kelly Oubre Jr. to set up a jumper near the free throw line." |
