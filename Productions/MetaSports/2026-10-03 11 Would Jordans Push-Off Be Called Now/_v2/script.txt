Michael Jordan pushed off {Bryon|BRY-un} Russell to win the 1998 Finals, and no foul was called. Now the NBA just rewrote how refs call that exact move. It's the Four F's: feeling, firming, force, and full extension. But if the arm hits with force, or fully extends, it's an offensive foul. ESPN says no, because he didn't use force or fully extend his arm. But the league's own examples are Jaylen Brown shoving Kelly {Oubre|OO-bray} in the chest, and RJ Barrett knocking Evan Mobley backward. Both are fouls now. So under the new rule, Jordan's shot is clean, but Jaylen Brown's isn't.
