Michael Jordan pushed off Bryon Russell to win the 1998 Finals, and no foul was called. Now the NBA is cracking down on push-offs. Feel and firm are legal. Force and full extension are fouls. So would Jordan's be called today? ESPN says no. But that's not even the crazy part. One of the league's own foul examples is Jaylen Brown, shoving Kelly Oubre in the chest. Brown was called for ten offensive fouls in one playoff series, twice as many as anyone else. Then the NBA fined him {$50,000|fifty thousand dollars} for criticizing the refs. So by ESPN's read, Jordan's push-off is legal, and Jaylen Brown's is the league's example.
