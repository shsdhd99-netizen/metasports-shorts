The Pacers only had six players on Monday night, and they almost won. Eleven Pacers sat out, hurt or resting. Three of the six who played were on two-way deals. The players who sat out make about {$193 million|a hundred and ninety-three million dollars}. The six who played? About {$15 million|fifteen million}. All six played at least thirty-five minutes. Every one of them scored in double figures. With a minute and a half left, they were down two. They lost by four. But no team had used so few players this early in a season since {1948|nineteen forty-eight}.
