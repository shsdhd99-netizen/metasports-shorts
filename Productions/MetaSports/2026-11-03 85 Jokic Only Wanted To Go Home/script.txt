Told his title parade was Thursday, Nikola Jokic said no. He was worried it would make him miss his family's horse races in Serbia. But at the parade, he told Denver he did not want to leave it. Six days after the title, he was at the track in Sombor, in a Dream Catcher shirt. He watched every race his family entered, and answered no reporters. He said no to the parade. Never to the horses.
