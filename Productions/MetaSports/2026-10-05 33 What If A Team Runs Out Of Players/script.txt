He fouled out, but the rule says he can't leave. February twenty fourteen, Lakers at Cleveland. L.A. had eight players available. Nick Young got hurt. Chris Kaman fouled out. Jordan Farmar cramped up. That left five. Then Robert Sacre picked up his sixth foul. But rule three says no team may be reduced to less than five players. So he stayed in, and the Lakers were hit with a technical foul. Any more fouls, and it would cost another technical every time. He never fouled again, and L.A. won a hundred nineteen to a hundred eight.
