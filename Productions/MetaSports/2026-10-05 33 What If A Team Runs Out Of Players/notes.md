# #33 What If A Team Runs Out Of Players (2026-10-05)

Lane: rule oddity with a real case. Concept: a player who fouls out must stay in if the bench is empty.
Added value: our own timeline of the one real case (Lakers at Cavaliers, Feb 5 2014) matched to the rulebook text; the box score proves each name.

## What is actually new? (premise audit)
Nothing is new; it is an evergreen rule explainer. Wording re-checked today against the official 2025-26 NBA Playing Rules PDF (https://cdn.nba.com/manage/2026/01/Official-2025-26-NBA-Playing-Rules.pdf, Rule 3 Section I) and the live https://official.nba.com/rule-no-3-players-substitutes-and-coaches/ (same text, Oct 5 2026). A 2026-27 PDF was not found; no change spotted.
Correction to the idea-bank outline ("charged a technical for each further foul"): rule text is: sixth foul + no subs -> player "shall remain in the game and shall be charged with a personal and team foul. A technical foul also shall be assessed against his team. All subsequent personal fouls, including offensive fouls, shall be treated similarly." The script says exactly that (technical against the team, again per further foul). The rulebook has NO forfeit for fewer than five (only: min 8 dressed, 9 in playoffs; Rule 12 forfeit language is about leaving the court after ejection). Not used in the script.
