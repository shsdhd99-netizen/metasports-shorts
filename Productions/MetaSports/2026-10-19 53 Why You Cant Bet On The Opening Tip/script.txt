This season, nobody can bet on the opening tip of an NBA game. The league just banned it. Same for betting on a player to miss his next shot, and on unders for players expected to play only a few minutes. Why? Jontay Porter. In March 2024, he played less than three minutes, said he felt sick, and left. A bettor had put {$80,000|eighty thousand dollars} on him coming up short. It would have paid {$1.1 million|one point one million dollars}. The NBA banned Porter for life. And this season, that kind of parlay is gone too. Right along with the opening tip.
