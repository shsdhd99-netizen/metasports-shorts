Sixteen Lakers, fifteen spots, and one of them is Bronny James. LeBron left for Philadelphia. Bronny stayed. At his first media day without his dad, he said: "Feels like he's not here." But the pressure is real. His two point three million is guaranteed, yet he averaged two point nine points in forty-two games. The Lakers reportedly must trim one player by October twenty-first. And his answer was about his name. "You guys know me as Bronny. My real name is LeBron." Fifteen spots. Without LeBron around, does Bronny earn one: yes or no?
