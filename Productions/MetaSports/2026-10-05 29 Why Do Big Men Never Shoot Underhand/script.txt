Knicks center Mitchell Robinson made twenty-nine percent of his playoff free throws. A Hall of Famer offered to fix it. Underhand. Robinson said, nah, silly as hell. That Hall of Famer is Rick Barry. Ninety percent for his career, fourth best in NBA history. Wilt Chamberlain used it the night he scored a hundred. Twenty-eight of thirty-two. Then he quit, because he felt like a sissy. Shaq shot fifty-three percent. His answer to Barry: I'd rather be a terrible free throw shooter who's in the Hall of Fame in fifteen minutes. Thanks, but no thanks.
