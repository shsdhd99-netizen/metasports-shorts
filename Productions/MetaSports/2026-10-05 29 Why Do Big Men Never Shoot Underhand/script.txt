Wilt Chamberlain scored a hundred points shooting free throws underhand. He made twenty-eight of thirty-two. That season he shot sixty-one percent, up from fifty. Then he quit, because he felt like a sissy. Rick Barry never quit. Ninety percent for his career, fourth best in NBA history. Last season, Knicks center Mitchell Robinson made twenty-nine percent in the playoffs. Asked about going underhand, he said, nah, silly as hell. Shaq shot fifty-three percent. His answer to Barry: I'd rather be a terrible free throw shooter who's in the Hall of Fame in fifteen minutes. Thanks, but no thanks.
