The last time the Knicks won a title, the NBA didn't even have a three-point line. That was 1973. You see, for 53 years, New York waited. Then this June, they finally did it, beating the Spurs in five, with Jalen Brunson dropping 45 in the clincher. And on October 20th, they raise the banner at the Garden. But the NBA gave them the hardest night possible. Their opponent is the new-look Sixers, with LeBron James and Jaylen Brown making their debuts. 53 years of waiting, and on the first night of the celebration, they have to stop LeBron.
