Only two NBA players are still allowed to wear number six. One is {24|twenty-four}. The other is thirty-eight. Bill Russell won eleven titles, so in 2022 the league retired six for every team. But players already wearing it kept it. {Jaylin|Jay-lin} Williams, of the Thunder, says his dad picked it. The league retired six a month later.
