Kawhi Leonard just got the bounce again. Same arena, same corner, seven years later. In 2019, his shot from that corner bounced on the rim four times, then dropped. It beat the Sixers at the buzzer of Game 7. No Game 7 had ever ended on a winning buzzer-beater before. Now, in his first night back in a Raptors jersey, a corner three from almost the same spot hit the rim, bounced, and fell in. So did it feel like déjà vu? "No, not at all. A lot of my shots do that."
