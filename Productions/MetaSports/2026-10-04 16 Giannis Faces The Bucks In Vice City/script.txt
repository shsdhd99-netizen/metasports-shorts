Thirteen years in Milwaukee. Zero games against the Bucks. Until November eighteenth. And he won't be playing for Miami. For one night only, the Heat trade Miami for Vice City: a new court, a new uniform, live on ESPN, the night before Grand Theft Auto six. After the trade, Giannis watched a video from Bucks fans and got emotional. But Milwaukee will always be my home. He's not the only one going back. Bobby Portis faces Milwaukee. And the four players the Heat sent for him, Herro, Ware, Jaquez and Jakucionis, face Miami. Six players. Two old teams. One city that doesn't exist.
