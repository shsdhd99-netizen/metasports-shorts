The NBA Cup final is going to a gym built in nineteen twenty-eight. Hinkle Fieldhouse seats about nine thousand. The first three Cup finals were all in Las Vegas. But Hinkle was never small. It opened with fifteen thousand seats, the biggest basketball arena in America until nineteen fifty. Tiny Milan beat Muncie here in nineteen fifty-four, the real Hoosiers game. Oscar Robertson won two state titles here. Brad Stevens coached Butler from here to two Final Fours. Now the NBA is coming. December eleventh, winner takes the Cup, in what was once the biggest gym in America.
