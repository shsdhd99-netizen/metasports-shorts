# #28 Why Is The NBA Cup At Hinkle (2026-10-05)

Lane: spectacle / place oddity. Concept: NBA Cup final goes to a 1928 gym that was once America's biggest.
Added value: the 15,000-seat / biggest-arena-until-1950 history (NBA.com) set against today's 9,100 seats; Hoosiers, Oscar, Stevens links.

## What is actually new?
Primary sources: ESPN Aug 12 2026 Cup schedule page (https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/49577473/2026-nba-season-tournament-cup-format-highlights-updates): group play Oct 30 - Nov 27, champion decided at Hinkle; NBA.com June 30 2026 (https://www.nba.com/news/hinkle-fieldhouse-will-host-emirates-nba-cup-championship). Nothing new since July; hook is the Cup tipping off Oct 30 (post by Oct 30 or Dec 4). No "only/first/last" claim in the script except "first three finals in Las Vegas" (ESPN, Jul 1 2026).
