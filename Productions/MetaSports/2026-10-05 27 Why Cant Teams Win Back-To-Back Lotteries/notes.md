# #27 Why Cant Teams Win Back-To-Back Lotteries (2026-10-05)

Lane: rule/ban oddity with a history payoff. Concept: the new lottery bans back-to-back No. 1 picks; Orlando (1992-93) and Cleveland (2013-14) already did it; the Wizards are the first team the ban touches. Added value: the two real back-to-back winners with their odds, and the Wizards consequence (derived from the rule text).

## What is actually new? (premise audit, Oct 5 2026)
Primary source: NBA release "NBA Board of Governors approves new Draft Lottery system to address tanking", May 28, 2026 (https://pr.nba.com/nba-board-of-governors-approves-new-draft-lottery-system-to-address-tanking): "The new system will take effect beginning with the 2027 NBA Draft." Pick restrictions: "No team's pick will be permitted to be the first pick in two consecutive NBA Drafts or a top-five pick in three consecutive NBA Drafts." Also in effect for the 2027, 2028 and 2029 drafts only; 2030 onward decided by a Board vote. The rule is NOT brand new (approved May 28, season start is the news peg), so the Short says "Now the NBA has banned it", not "just". The rule has not yet applied to any lottery (first one is May 2027).
