Bones Hyland just got a technical for a fake throw. The Wolves led Toronto by 25 with under two minutes left. Bones got called for a foul, grabbed the ball and cocked it back like he'd fire it at the ref. The ref said something. So Bones did it again, grinning. Whistle. Technical. His eyes went huge. Then, while Toronto shot the free throw, he and the ref were already chatting, all smiles.
