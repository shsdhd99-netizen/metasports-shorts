The Spurs led the NBA Finals by twenty-nine points. And lost. They hit a Finals record fourteen threes in the first half, and led by twenty-seven at the break. Then the Knicks started chasing. San Antonio went three for seventeen from deep in the second half. Since nineteen ninety-seven, nobody had come back from more than twenty-four down in a Finals game. Then Brunson launched a long three to win it, and missed. But OG Anunoby tipped it in with one point two seconds left. A hundred seven to a hundred six. The biggest comeback in Finals history.
