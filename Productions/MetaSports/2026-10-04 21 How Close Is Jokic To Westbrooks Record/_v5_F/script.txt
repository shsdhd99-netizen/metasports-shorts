Russell Westbrook needed eighteen seasons to set the triple-double record. Nikola Jokic could take it before Christmas. Westbrook retired this summer with two hundred and nine. Jokic has one ninety-eight, in almost five hundred fewer games. Last season, he got one almost every other night. At that pace, the twelve he needs take about twenty-three games, and Denver's twenty-seventh game is Christmas Day, at Golden State. Last Christmas, he dropped fifty-six, sixteen and fifteen, a line nobody in NBA history had ever posted. This Christmas, the record could already be his.
