# #26 How Is A Screen Suddenly Illegal Now (2026-10-05)

Lane: rule/ban/"caught". Concept: screeners who shove are fouls; the NBA's own video shows where the line is. Added value: all four example fouls plus the one legal "play-on" screen are from the NBA's own referee video, and the Short states the line in the league's own words (affects the defender's ability to play defense).

## What is actually new? (premise audit, Oct 5 2026)
Primary source: NBA Official 2026-27 Points of Emphasis Part 1 video (X https://x.com/NBAOfficial/status/2104934304845287701, 2026-09-29 14:00 UTC; page https://official.nba.com/2026-27-points-of-emphasis/ "Freedom of Movement - Screens"). The screen rule itself is NOT new: Bleacher Report (Sep 29, 2026): "enforcing rules already in the book rather than introducing new ones". So the Short never says the rule is new or "suddenly illegal"; the hook says a screen is legal, shoving is a foul, and the NBA showed refs the line. The idea-bank title ("suddenly") is only an upload-title option, see UPLOAD.md.
