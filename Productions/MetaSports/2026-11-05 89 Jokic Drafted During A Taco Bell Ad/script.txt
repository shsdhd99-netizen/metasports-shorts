Nikola Jokic was drafted during a Taco Bell commercial. Pick forty-one showed up only as a name at the bottom of the screen. Jokic says he has never eaten Taco Bell because of it. But now he has more than two hundred triple-doubles. Only Russell Westbrook has more. Westbrook needed eleven seventy-five games to reach two hundred. Jokic did it in eight fourteen. The pick nobody watched is chasing the record.
