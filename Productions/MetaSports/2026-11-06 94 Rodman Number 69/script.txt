The NBA banned Dennis Rodman's jersey number. When Dallas signed him, he asked for sixty-nine. The Mavericks printed one. The league said no. So he wore seventy. Mark Cuban even let him live in his guest house. The Mavs had just won ten of thirteen. With Rodman, they won four of twelve. He was ejected twice. Five weeks after he arrived, they released him. The only thing Dallas kept was the jersey he never wore.
