Caitlin Clark did something no NBA player has ever done. 45 points and 10 assists, in under 30 minutes. It was also the first 40-and-10 game in WNBA history. Thirty seasons, and nobody had done it. But here's the crazy part. Clark has had at least six games with 30 points and 10 assists. Nobody else in WNBA history has done it more than once. And that same night, she became the fastest player in WNBA history to 200 threes: 74 games. The old record was 81. 45 and 10, in 29 minutes. No NBA player has ever done that.
