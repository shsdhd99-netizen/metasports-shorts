The oldest triple-double in NBA history lasted twenty-two years. Karl Malone owned it, at forty years, a hundred twenty-seven days old, back in two thousand three. Then LeBron James broke it in February. Forty-one years, forty-four days. He broke his own record again in March. Forty-one years, seventy-nine days. Eleven days later, a third time. Forty-one years, ninety days. Three records in forty-six days. Opening night is October twentieth, at the Knicks. LeBron will be forty-one years, two hundred ninety-four days old. Any triple-double he records from now on is the oldest ever.
