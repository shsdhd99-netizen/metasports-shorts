# The story format (from Ahmed's network, measured Oct 21, 2026)

**Evidence:**
- 3,154 Shorts across Ahmed's Goal (424K subs, median 276K views), Ahmed's Basketball (121K, median 159K) and Ahmeds Business (86.6K, median 49K).
- 19 transcripts; 7 Shorts watched frame by frame.
- Full write-ups in `Research Notes/Story Format Study - Ahmed (Oct 21, 2026).md` and `Research Notes/Ahmed's Network and a Second Channel (Oct 21, 2026).md`.

**Tiers:** [V] measured, [S] seen in a few, [H] my reading.

## Script
- [V] **Line 1 is the title**, read word for word: "Players who refused to play in matches." (19 of 19)
- [V] **"Back in [year], [famous name]..."** or "The year was..." opens the story (12 of 19).
- [V] **One story per Short**, even though the title sounds like a list.
- [V] **A turn every 8-12 s:** "But...", "There was just one problem", "But it gets even crazier", "But then came the moment nobody saw coming". Use 2-4 escalations.
- [V] **Payoff:** "Here's the wild part / the twist", then a hard number or irony. Never a question.
- [V] **Optional teaser:** "And my next video is about X. Follow if you want the algorithm to show it to you." Seen in 2 of the top 6 Goal Shorts and in most TikTok copies.
- [V] **Length and pace:**
  - runtime median 70-76 s (58-167 s);
  - 200-330 words at 228-282 wpm;
  - the voice starts at 0.00 and runs to the last frame.
- [V] **Asides:** "You see,", "The thing is,", and first-person lines ("Honestly, I think...").

## Pictures
- [V] **A new picture every 1.1-2.1 s.** The first picture holds about 2.5 s under the title card (0-1 cuts in the first 3 s).
- [V] **Literal matching:** the named person is on screen when named. Abstract phrases get a literal picture.
- [V] **Framing:** most shots are cropped full-frame. Wide clips sit over a blurred copy of themselves (our `--blurfit`).
- [V] **Ending:** the last picture shows the payoff, or the next video's subject when it is teased.
- [V] **Rough edges they accept:** soft 480p archive and watermarks. We don't copy the watermarks or other creators' captions.

## Captions (measured on their frames; our `--caption-style ahmed`)
- TikTok Sans SemiBold, size 80 at 1080x1920.
- White, with a thin black outline (3.2) and no box.
- Centred at y=960.
- 2-3 words a card, sentence case as spoken, punctuation kept.
- The first card is the whole title on 1-2 lines with 1-2 CAPS words (`title_caption.txt`).

## Audio
- [V] Mono-centred narration.
- [V] A continuous quiet music bed about 20 dB under the voice: their noise floor never drops below about -45 dB, and YouTube's captions show "[music]".

## Packaging and business
- [V] **Title** = line 1, 6-11 words, sentence case (older ALL CAPS). No description, no tags, no hashtags.
- [V] **Series:** a title that works is reused for new stories ("Episode 2...12"), seen on 141 Goal and 126 Basketball titles.
- [V] **Cadence:** about 2 a day per channel, batch-scheduled. When they pushed to about 3 a day, medians fell.
- [V] **Distribution:** the same file goes to TikTok and Instagram (every video is over 1 minute). YouTube auto-dubbing is on.
- [V] **Sponsors:** read inside the script (Polymarket, #PolyPartner on TikTok).
